Gcore Streaming uses a PAYG (Pay-As-You-Go) tariff for self-service customers. New PAYG accounts start with a 14-day trial. Enterprise accounts may operate under custom plans with negotiated terms.

Streaming-specific plans and limits are described below. The [billing&nbsp;overview](/account-settings/billing/overview) covers account-level billing cycles, invoices, payments, and overages. [Billing&nbsp;reports](/streaming/billing/usage-and-billing-reports) show actual Streaming consumption.

## Start with the PAYG trial

The trial lasts 14 days and is available once per account. It cannot be restarted after it has been used.

The trial provides limited quotas for video storage, transcoding, delivery, AI services, and live streaming.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/docs/streaming/billing/overview-of-tariffs/streaming-trial-parameters.png" alt="Streaming sidebar showing trial status with video upload and transcoding limits" width="70%" />
</Frame>

When the account reaches a trial limit, the [Gcore Customer Portal](https://portal.gcore.com) shows an alert and a tooltip. To continue testing with a higher trial quota, contact support or an account manager.

## Move to active PAYG

Adding a payment method before the trial ends moves the account to active PAYG automatically. Further processing is billed under the paid tariff from that point.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/docs/streaming/billing/overview-of-tariffs/streaming-billing-payg-tariff.png" alt="Select Streaming plan dialog with PAYG option highlighted" width="70%" />
</Frame>

If the trial expires without a payment method, the account moves to Trial end status. To resume Streaming, a new paid activation is required.

<Note>
To activate PAYG before the trial ends without adding a payment method, contact the support team. The support team can switch the account to active state manually.
</Note>

### Trial and active PAYG limits

Active PAYG removes most trial restrictions. The differences are shown below:

| Resource | Trial PAYG | Active PAYG |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Video storage | 30 minutes available. Uploading videos decreases the available volume, while deleting videos restores it up to the 30-minute trial limit. | No hard limit. A soft limit of 100,000 minutes (~5 TB) applies by default and can be increased by contacting support or an account manager. |
| Transcoding | 30 minutes available. VOD and live stream transcoding consume this volume permanently; completed transcodes do not return minutes to the trial quota. | No limit. |
| Delivery | 1,000 minutes. | No limit. |
| AI services | 5,000 minutes. | No limit. |
| Live streams | One active live stream during the trial. The Customer Portal shows an alert when the account reaches this limit. | No hard limit. A soft limit of three active live streams at one time applies by default and can be increased by contacting support or an account manager. |

## Custom enterprise plans

Enterprise customers may operate under custom plans with tailored pricing models and service conditions. Custom plans can include a committed monthly volume at a committed price. If actual usage exceeds this committed volume, additional consumption is billed at overlimit rates.

Trial terms for enterprise accounts can be extended by duration or available quota. Contact the assigned account manager to request the required extension.

<Note>
If the account is suspended, video assets remain stored for up to one month. Restoring the account within this period reactivates the video library without data loss. General suspension rules are in the [billing&nbsp;overview](/account-settings/billing/overview).
</Note>
