The Statistics tab counts CDN delivery for one video from any player, and the figures can lag by up to 5 minutes.

## Statistics for a video

Open the video, then read the charts on the **Statistics** tab.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the video list">
    In the [Gcore&nbsp;Customer&nbsp;Portal](https://portal.gcore.com), navigate to **Streaming** > **Video Hosting** > **All videos**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open statistics">
    In the video list, click the video name and then select the **Statistics** tab. Or open the three-dot menu and click **Statistics**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Use the date range, time zone, and **Auto refresh** controls above the charts to change the view. The default range is the last 24 hours, auto refresh is off or every 30 seconds or 1, 2, 3, 4, or 5 minutes, and the time zone is **UTC** or **Local time zone**.

## Delivery minutes

**Delivery minutes** is how many minutes of the video the CDN delivered during the selected dates. A player that downloads more of the file than the viewer watches increases this number.

## Unique viewers

Each viewer is counted only once, regardless of how many times they connect or disconnect or which player they use.
