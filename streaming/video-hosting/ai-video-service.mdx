Gcore Video Streaming offers advanced video processing features for both video on demand (VOD) and live streaming:

- [AI&nbsp;subtitles](/streaming/video-hosting/ai-for-video/generate-ai-subtitles-and-add-them-to-video). AI Automated Speech Recognition (AI ASR) generates and translates subtitles in a wide range of languages, making video content accessible to global audiences.
- [Content&nbsp;moderation](/streaming/ai-video-service/content-moderation). Videos are analyzed for nudity, offensive language, or violence to confirm that content is safe and appropriate for audiences.

## AI video services infrastructure

Gcore uses its own infrastructure to execute AI models, keeping all data on EU and US servers and minimizing the risk of data leaks.

The unified infrastructure connects to other Gcore AI-powered solutions, including [AI&nbsp;GPU&nbsp;Cloud](https://gcore.com/cloud/ai-gpu) for machine learning acceleration and [FastEdge](https://gcore.com/fastedge) for low-latency serverless edge computing.

## Activate AI video services

AI services are available to all Streaming customers via the [Gcore Customer Portal](https://portal.gcore.com) and API. If access to AI is unavailable, contact [Gcore&nbsp;support](mailto:support@gcore.com). General usage of the AI video service is free; translation may incur charges — details are on the [pricing&nbsp;page](https://gcore.com/pricing/streaming-platform).

## Available features

Subtitle generation and translation are available for VOD videos via the API.

## Content moderation

The [API&nbsp;documentation](/api-reference/streaming/ai/create-ai-task) covers integration for each task type.

For one-off checks on individual videos, the [content&nbsp;moderation guide](/streaming/ai-video-service/content-moderation) describes the Customer Portal workflow.

Gcore Streaming offers five AI content moderation tasks — four related to nudity and one for sports.

### Content unsafe for work

The **nsfw** task is the strictest nudity detection task, as it identifies and filters videos containing inappropriately clothed people (i.e., they don’t have to be fully or partially nude to get flagged). The algorithm flags content as NSFW if it is potentially unsuitable for viewing at work or in public places. If NSFW content is detected, the AI model provides its confidence level as a percentage.

### Soft nudity

The **soft_nudity** task is less strict than the **nsfw** task, as it only flags videos with full or partial nudity. It detects explicit and partial nudity, including videos of male and female faces and other uncovered body parts. Use it to determine whether content is appropriate for all audiences or should be blocked. The check returns information about the object identified, the video frame number where it was found, and the probability of the detected object. The response only includes objects with at least a 30% confidence level.

This task identifies faces and other body parts and detects more objects than hard nudity detection, so analyzing a video for soft nudity takes more time.

### Hard nudity

The **hard_nudity** task is less strict than the **soft_nudity** task, as it only detects exposed primary and secondary sex organs in a video. Use it to determine whether publishing a video to all audiences is appropriate or whether it should be blocked. If hard nudity content is detected, the AI model returns its confidence level as a percentage.

This task detects fewer objects than **soft_nudity**, so it works faster when detecting exposed body parts only.

### Sport activities

The **sport** task identifies various sporting activities in a video, including specific types, actions, events, and moments. Use it to determine whether particular sports were filmed or to locate crucial scenes — goal shots in football or dunks in a basketball game.

The check returns information about the object identified, the video frame number where it was found, and the probability of the detected object. Objects with a probability of at least 30% are included in the response.
