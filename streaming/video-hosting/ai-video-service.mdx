Gcore Video Streaming provides two AI-powered capabilities for VOD processing: subtitle generation and content moderation. Both integrate via the REST API; content moderation also has a Customer Portal interface for one-off checks.

| Capability | What it does | Access |
|------------|--------------|--------|
| [AI&nbsp;subtitles](/streaming/video-hosting/ai-for-video/generate-ai-subtitles-and-add-them-to-video) | Generates and translates subtitles using Automated Speech Recognition (ASR) | API |
| [Content&nbsp;moderation](/streaming/ai-video-service/content-moderation) | Analyzes video content for nudity and sports-related objects | API, Customer Portal |

## Access and pricing

AI services are available to all Streaming customers. General usage is free; translation may incur charges — details are on the [pricing&nbsp;page](https://gcore.com/pricing/streaming-platform). If access to AI is unavailable, contact [Gcore&nbsp;support](mailto:support@gcore.com).

<Warning>
Live streaming subtitle generation is not supported. Both capabilities are available for VOD only.
</Warning>

## AI subtitles

[AI&nbsp;subtitle&nbsp;generation](/streaming/video-hosting/ai-for-video/generate-ai-subtitles-and-add-them-to-video) uses ASR to transcribe speech and translate it into supported languages. It produces subtitle tracks that can be embedded in the video player or exported as separate files.

## Content moderation

[Content&nbsp;moderation](/streaming/ai-video-service/content-moderation) analyzes VOD content and returns detection results with confidence scores. The [API&nbsp;documentation](/api-reference/streaming/ai/create-ai-task) covers task submission. For one-off checks on individual videos, the [content&nbsp;moderation guide](/streaming/ai-video-service/content-moderation) describes the Customer Portal workflow.

Gcore Streaming provides four content moderation task types. Select based on the content categories to detect and the required strictness:

| Task | Detects | Strictness | When to use |
|------|---------|------------|-------------|
| **nsfw** | Inappropriately clothed people — flagged regardless of degree of clothing | Highest | General workplace-appropriate content screening |
| **soft_nudity** | Full or partial nudity; returns body-part object classes and frame positions | Medium | Publishing decisions for mixed-audience platforms |
| **hard_nudity** | Exposed primary and secondary sex organs only | Lower | Fast checks when only explicit content matters |
| **sport** | Sporting activities, events, and key moments | N/A | Sports content indexing and highlights detection |

All tasks return the detected object class, the video frame number, and the confidence level as a percentage. Results with a confidence level below 30% are excluded from the response.

`soft_nudity` detects a wider range of object classes than `hard_nudity` and takes longer to process. `nsfw` is the strictest option and covers cases where subjects are clothed but the content is inappropriate for professional or public environments.
