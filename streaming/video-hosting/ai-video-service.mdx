Gcore Video Streaming provides two AI capabilities for VOD assets: subtitle generation and content moderation. Both are available via the [REST&nbsp;API](/api-reference/streaming/ai/create-ai-task); content moderation also has a [Gcore&nbsp;Customer&nbsp;Portal](https://portal.gcore.com/streaming/ai/list) interface. General usage is free; translation may incur [additional&nbsp;charges](https://gcore.com/pricing/streaming-platform).

| Capability | What it does | Access |
|------------|--------------|--------|
| [AI&nbsp;subtitles](/streaming/video-hosting/ai-for-video/generate-ai-subtitles-and-add-them-to-video) | Transcribes audio and translates subtitles into 100+ languages using Automated Speech Recognition (ASR) | API |
| Content&nbsp;moderation | Analyzes video and image content for nudity and sports activities | API, Customer Portal |

## Content moderation

Content moderation provides four tasks. Three target nudity and form a scale from broadest to narrowest: [NSFW&nbsp;materials](/streaming/ai-video-service/content-moderation/nsfw-detection) flags content as potentially inappropriate even without explicit nudity; [Soft&nbsp;nudity](/streaming/ai-video-service/content-moderation/soft-nudity-detection) covers full or partial nudity across a wider range of body-part categories; [Hard&nbsp;nudity](/streaming/ai-video-service/ai-nudity-detection) is limited to exposed primary and secondary sex organs. The fourth task, [Sport&nbsp;activities](/streaming/ai-video-service/content-moderation/sport-detection), is separate — it identifies sporting activities, events, and key moments in video content.

### Analysis process

Regardless of the selected task, video analysis runs on keyframes (iFrames) at the interval defined during encoding. The service therefore does not detect objects that appear only between keyframes. Gcore processes static images as one-second assets and deletes the original file from storage after processing.

### Results and confidence

Each task returns the detected object class, the video frame number, and a confidence percentage; the service excludes results below 30%. The confidence score represents the probability that a detected object belongs to a specific category — there is no universal cutoff that applies across all services, and a threshold appropriate for one context may be too strict or too permissive for another.
