Gcore Video Streaming provides AI-powered subtitle generation and content moderation for VOD assets. Both capabilities are available via the REST API; content moderation also has a Customer Portal interface for one-off checks. General usage is free; translation may incur additional charges — details are on the [pricing&nbsp;page](https://gcore.com/pricing/streaming-platform). If access to AI is unavailable, contact [Gcore&nbsp;support](mailto:support@gcore.com).

| Capability | What it does | Access |
|------------|--------------|--------|
| [AI&nbsp;subtitles](/streaming/video-hosting/ai-for-video/generate-ai-subtitles-and-add-them-to-video) | Generates and translates subtitles using Automated Speech Recognition (ASR) | API |
| [Content&nbsp;moderation](/streaming/ai-video-service/content-moderation) | Analyzes video content for nudity and sports-related objects | API, Customer Portal |

Content moderation provides four task types — select based on the content categories to detect:

| Task | Detects | Strictness |
|------|---------|------------|
| **nsfw** | Inappropriately clothed people — flagged regardless of degree of clothing | Highest |
| **soft_nudity** | Full or partial nudity across a wider range of body-part object classes than hard nudity | Medium |
| **hard_nudity** | Exposed primary and secondary sex organs only | Lower |
| **sport** | Sporting activities, events, and key moments | — |

All tasks return the detected object class, the video frame number, and the confidence level as a percentage. Results below 30% confidence are excluded from the response. `soft_nudity` detects a wider range of object classes than `hard_nudity` and takes longer to process.
