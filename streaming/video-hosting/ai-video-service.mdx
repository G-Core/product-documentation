Gcore Video Streaming provides two AI capabilities for VOD assets: subtitle generation and content moderation. Both are available via the [REST&nbsp;API](/api-reference/streaming/ai/create-ai-task); content moderation also has a [Customer&nbsp;Portal](https://portal.gcore.com/streaming/ai/list) interface. General usage is free; translation may incur additional charges — details are on the [pricing&nbsp;page](https://gcore.com/pricing/streaming-platform). If access to AI is unavailable, contact [Gcore&nbsp;support](mailto:support@gcore.com).

| Capability | What it does | Access |
|------------|--------------|--------|
| [AI&nbsp;subtitles](/streaming/video-hosting/ai-for-video/generate-ai-subtitles-and-add-them-to-video) | Transcribes audio and translates subtitles into 100+ languages using Automated Speech Recognition (ASR) | API |
| Content&nbsp;moderation | Analyzes video and image files for nudity and sports-related objects; returns detected objects with confidence scores | API, Customer Portal |

## Detection tasks

Content moderation provides four task types. The three nudity checks differ in scope: [NSFW&nbsp;materials](/streaming/ai-video-service/content-moderation/nsfw-detection) applies the broadest classification and can flag content even without explicit nudity; [Soft&nbsp;nudity](/streaming/ai-video-service/content-moderation/soft-nudity-detection) focuses on full or partial nudity across a wider range of body-part categories; and [Hard&nbsp;nudity](/streaming/ai-video-service/ai-nudity-detection) is limited to exposed primary and secondary sex organs.

[Sport&nbsp;activities](/streaming/ai-video-service/content-moderation/sport-detection) serves a different purpose, identifying sporting activities, events, and key moments in video content.

## Analysis process

Content moderation processes video by analyzing keyframes (iFrames). The analysis frequency matches the keyframe interval set in the video encoding — objects between keyframes are not detected. Static images are also supported; image duration is treated as 1 second. After processing, the original file is deleted from Gcore storage.

Each task returns the detected object class, the video frame number, and a confidence percentage. Results with confidence below 30% are excluded by the service.

## Confidence scores

The confidence score is a probability value. There is no universal cutoff that applies across all services — a threshold appropriate for one context may be too strict or too permissive for another. Process a representative sample of videos and analyze the resulting score distribution to determine an appropriate cutoff before applying content filtering in production.
