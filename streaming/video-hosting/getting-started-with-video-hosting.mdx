## Publish a video

To publish a video:

1. [Upload the video](/streaming/video-hosting/upload-a-video-and-embed-it-to-your-app) from a local file, a remote URL, or a Google Drive account. Gcore transcodes the source automatically — adaptive renditions at SD, HD, Full HD, 2K, and 4K are packaged as HLS MPEG-TS, HLS CMAF, MPEG-DASH CMAF, and MP4.
2. The [VOD&nbsp;status](/streaming/video-hosting/vod-status) page shows transcoding progress — renditions become available as each quality level completes.
3. Embed the hosted player using the iframe code, or use the HLS or MPEG-DASH manifest URL in a custom player.

[Subtitles](/streaming/video-hosting/subtitles-and-closed-captions-for-vod) and closed captions, [multiple&nbsp;audio&nbsp;tracks](/streaming/video-hosting/multi-audio), [AES-128 encryption](/streaming/video-hosting/protect-your-videos-with-the-aes-128-encryption), [viewer statistics](/streaming/statistics/statistics-of-views), and [VOD2Live simulation](/streaming/video-hosting/create-an-illusion-of-a-live-broadcast-with-uploaded-videos) extend the basic workflow. For large libraries, [multi-codec VOD](/streaming/video-hosting/multi-codec) reduces CDN traffic at comparable visual quality.
