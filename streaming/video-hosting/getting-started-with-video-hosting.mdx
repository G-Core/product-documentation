## Start here

To publish the first video:

1. [Upload the video](/streaming/video-hosting/upload-a-video-and-embed-it-to-your-app) from a local file, a remote URL, or a Google Drive account. Transcoding starts automatically.
2. Wait for transcoding to complete — [VOD status](/streaming/video-hosting/vod-status) shows progress. Adaptive renditions at SD, HD, Full HD, 2K, and 4K are generated automatically.
3. Embed the hosted player in a page using the iframe code, or use the HLS or MPEG-DASH manifest URL in a custom player.

## What Gcore handles

After upload, Gcore Video Hosting:

- Transcodes the source into adaptive bitrate (ABR) renditions at multiple quality levels, up to 8K.
- Packages renditions as HLS MPEG-TS, HLS CMAF, MPEG-DASH CMAF, and MP4.
- Delivers content from 210+ CDN edge locations worldwide.
- Hosts a built-in player embeddable in any page via an iframe.

For large libraries, [HEVC, AV1, and VP9 multi-codec VOD](/streaming/video-hosting/multi-codec) reduces CDN traffic at comparable visual quality.
