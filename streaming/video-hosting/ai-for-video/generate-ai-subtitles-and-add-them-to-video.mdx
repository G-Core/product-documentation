Gcore AI automated speech recognition ([ASR](https://gcore.com/streaming-platform/ai-for-video)) automatically generates [video&nbsp;captions](/streaming/video-hosting/subtitles-and-closed-captions-for-vod#what-are-subtitles-and-closed-captions) (also referred to as subtitles) using AI models. It supports two use cases:

  * **Transcription:** Generate video captions from the original audio. Our AI models transcribe the speech of one or multiple speakers, even if they use different languages.
  * **Translation:** Translate captions into more than 100 languages to make videos accessible to multilingual audiences.

## ASR processing

We use [Whisper&nbsp;ASR](https://openai.com/research/whisper) from OpenAI, along with a range of other specialized AI models. These models operate on Gcore infrastructure, so no files are transferred to external services. After processing, all original files are deleted from the AI system's local storage.


## Capabilities

Gcore ASR is designed for ease of integration across different video sources and workflows.

  * **Ease of use.** Generate ready-to-use subtitles in the [Gcore Customer Portal](https://portal.gcore.com) or with a few API requests.
  * **Supported languages.** AI recognizes and transcribes audio in over 100 languages worldwide. If the desired language is not listed, contact [Gcore&nbsp;support](mailto:support@gcore.com) to request it.
  * **Broad video support.** Generate video captions for any MP4 video uploaded to Gcore Video Hosting or stored externally, for instance, on AWS.
  * **Multi-language support.** We support recognition of multiple spoken languages in a single video (the "code-switching" feature), accounting for speakers who switch between languages.
