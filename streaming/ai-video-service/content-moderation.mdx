Gcore AI content moderation analyzes video and image content to detect nudity and sports activity. Detection results can be used for content filtering, age-gating, or sports event identification.

Supported content types:

  * [NSFW&nbsp;materials](/streaming/ai-video-service/content-moderation/nsfw-detection)
  * [Hard&nbsp;nudity](/streaming/ai-video-service/ai-nudity-detection)
  * [Soft&nbsp;nudity](/streaming/ai-video-service/content-moderation/soft-nudity-detection)
  * [Sport&nbsp;activities](/streaming/ai-video-service/content-moderation/sport-detection)

## Detection process

Gcore runs multiple AI models to analyze sensitive and restricted content types in real time. After processing, the original file is deleted from AI local storage.

The content is detected by analyzing keyframes (iFrames) in a video; at a two-second keyframe interval, analysis runs every two seconds. Objects between keyframe timestamps are not analyzed.

Static images are also supported; processing treats image duration as 1 second.

## Use cases

Video streaming and TV broadcasting:

  * Deliver age-appropriate content and comply with content service policies
  * Identify illegal or potentially violent content in real time

Broadcasting of sports events:

  * Identify and tag specific sports and key moments in video content
  * Build personalized content recommendations based on viewer preferences

Video on demand (VOD) platforms:

  * Block uploads of illegal materials
  * Streamline content review and improve its accuracy

## Assessing the probability of inappropriate content

There is no universal probability threshold that applies to all services. Different services cater to different audiences — an acceptable nudity score for an adult content service differs from one designed for children.

A probability threshold can be configured to match the requirements of a specific service. One approach is to process a representative sample of videos and analyze the resulting probability distribution.
