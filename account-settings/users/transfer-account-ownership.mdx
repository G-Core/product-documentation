Every account has one owner, initially the user who registered it. A person can own multiple accounts, and each account can have several [administrators](/account-settings/users/about-users).

An **Owner** badge identifies this person on the [Users](https://portal.gcore.com/accounts/settings/users) and [Access&nbsp;management](https://portal.gcore.com/accounts/settings/access-management/users) pages. The badge is visible to everyone in the account. Hovering it shows "Account owner. Cannot be removed. Ownership can only be transferred."

## Permissions

The Owner policy grants full access to IAM and Billing. It cannot be edited, deleted, or revoked from the owner.

Only this role can enable or disable mandatory [two-factor&nbsp;authentication](/account-settings/my-profile/two-factor-authentication) for the account. Before changing this setting, the owner must enable 2FA on their own profile.

The owner also submits KYC requests and receives status notifications.

## Ownership transfer

Only the current owner can transfer ownership. The recipient must be an active administrator of the same account.

1. In **Access management**, open the edit page of the administrator who will become the new owner and find **Ownership delegation** under **Access control**. This field is visible only to the current owner.

2. Select the administrator who will become the new owner.

3. Confirm the transfer using the verification method associated with the current account:

   - If 2FA is enabled, enter the one-time code, even if sign-in also uses SSO.
   - If 2FA is disabled and sign-in uses SSO or a social account, follow the link sent by email.
   - Otherwise, enter the account password.

After verification, the Owner policy moves to the selected administrator. The new owner receives an email notification but does not need to accept the transfer.

The previous owner remains in the account as a user or administrator. Until ownership is transferred, other administrators cannot remove or demote the current owner or take over the account.
