<p>Every account has one owner, the user who registered the account. Only that user can transfer ownership.</p>

## Account owner

<p>An account can have many <a href="/account-settings/users/about-users">administrators</a> and only one owner. The owner is a specific user, not another name for the administrator role.</p>

<p>One person can own several accounts. Each account keeps its own owner.</p>

## Reserved actions

<p>Full access to IAM and Billing comes from the Owner policy. That policy cannot be edited, deleted, or taken away from the owner.</p>

<p>Only the owner can turn mandatory <a href="/account-settings/my-profile/two-factor-authentication">two-factor&nbsp;authentication</a> on or off for the account. The owner must already have two-factor authentication enabled.</p>

<p>The owner creates KYC requests and receives status notifications for them.</p>

## Find the owner

<p>An <strong>Owner</strong> badge appears on the owner's row in the users table. Every user in the account can see the badge.</p>

<p>The same badge is on <a href="https://portal.gcore.com/accounts/settings/users">Users</a> and on the access-management users list. Hovering the badge shows "Account owner. Cannot be removed. Ownership can only be transferred."</p>

## Transfer ownership

<p>Only the current owner can start a transfer. The new owner must be an active administrator of the same account.</p>

<p>The previous owner stays in the account, as a user or as an administrator.</p>

## Removal and role changes

<p>An administrator cannot remove the owner or lower that user's role. Transfer ownership first.</p>

<p>An administrator cannot assign ownership to themselves.</p>
