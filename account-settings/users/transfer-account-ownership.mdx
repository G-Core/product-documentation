Every account has one owner. By default, the user who registers the account becomes its owner. Only the current owner can transfer ownership to another user.

## Owner and administrator

An account can have multiple [administrators](/account-settings/users/about-users), but only one owner. A user can own multiple accounts, with ownership managed separately for each account.

## Owner permissions

Unlike an administrator, the owner holds the Owner policy, which gives full access to IAM and Billing. It cannot be edited, deleted, or taken away from the owner.

Only the owner can turn mandatory [two-factor&nbsp;authentication](/account-settings/my-profile/two-factor-authentication) on or off for the account, after the owner enables it on their own profile.

The owner creates KYC requests and receives status notifications for them.

## Owner badge

In the [Gcore&nbsp;Customer&nbsp;Portal](https://portal.gcore.com), an **Owner** badge appears on the owner's row in the users table, and every user in the account can see it.

The badge is on [Users](https://portal.gcore.com/accounts/settings/users) and on [Access&nbsp;management](https://portal.gcore.com/accounts/settings/access-management/users). Hovering it shows "Account owner. Cannot be removed. Ownership can only be transferred."

## Ownership transfer

The new owner must be an active administrator of the same account, and the previous owner stays in the account.

**Ownership delegation** is on the **Access control** table of the user edit page in Access management, and only the current owner can see the field.

1. In **Ownership delegation**, open the list and select one user. A new selection clears the previous one.

2. Confirm the transfer using the verification method associated with the current owner's account:

   - When two-factor authentication is enabled, enter the one-time code. The code is used even when the owner also signs in with SSO.
   - When two-factor authentication is off and the owner signs in with SSO or a social account, open the link from the email message.
   - In other cases, enter the password.

The Owner policy moves to the selected user. The new owner receives an email message, and the transfer completes without a response from that user.

An administrator cannot remove the owner, lower that user's role, or take ownership, so the current owner transfers ownership first.
