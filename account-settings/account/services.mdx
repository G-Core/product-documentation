The [Gcore Customer Portal](https://portal.gcore.com) dashboard shows the status of every Gcore service on the account. Each service appears as its own block with a status badge — **Active** while the service is running.

<Frame>![Dashboard with a status badge on each service](/images/docs/account-settings/account/services/dashboard-service-status.png)</Frame>

## Disable or resume a service

The Customer Portal has no page for changing a service status. There is no **Disable service** or **Resume service** control in the dashboard, in the service sections, or in the account settings.

Before requesting that a service be disabled, note that its data is deleted after the applicable [suspension&nbsp;period](/account-settings/billing/data-deletion).

To disable a service or resume a stopped one, contact [Gcore&nbsp;Support](https://support.gcore.com/hc/en-us/requests/new).
