One Gcore login can belong to several accounts. The Customer Portal works with one account at a time, and the profile menu shows which account is active.

## Active account

The profile menu shows the account currently in use:

1. In the [Gcore Customer Portal](https://portal.gcore.com), click the avatar in the top-right corner.
2. Read the account name and the account ID in the menu. The email address of the login appears above them.

<Frame>![Profile menu showing the active account name and account ID](/images/docs/account-settings/account/additional-accounts/profile-menu-active-account.png)</Frame>

The same account name is editable on the [General&nbsp;information](/account-settings/account/overview) page, where the account ID is read-only.

## Create an additional account

Creating another account from the profile menu is not available in the Customer Portal. To get an additional account, contact [Gcore&nbsp;Support](https://support.gcore.com/hc/en-us/requests/new).

## Switch to another account

The profile menu does not list other accounts and has no control for changing the active one. Switching happens at sign-in:

1. In the profile menu, click **Sign out**. The [sign-in&nbsp;page](https://auth.gcore.com/login/signin) opens.
2. Sign in again. When the login belongs to more than one account, select the account to use. A login with a single account opens the Customer Portal directly.
