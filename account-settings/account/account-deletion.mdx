Deleting an account removes its services and personal data. The request can be canceled within 10 days; services stop immediately when the request is sent, and Gcore permanently deletes the account after that window.

## Deletion overview

The **Delete account** section on the General information page shows these terms before the request is sent.

<Warning>
Back up any data needed before requesting deletion.
</Warning>

Gcore sends a confirmation email when the deletion is complete.

## Deletion procedure

Submit the request from the General information page.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open account settings">
    In the [Gcore Customer Portal](https://portal.gcore.com), click the avatar in the top-right corner and select **Profile**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Delete account section">
    In the sidebar, navigate to **Account** > **General information** and scroll to the **Delete account** section. Click **Delete my account**.

    <Frame>![Delete my account button in the General information section](/images/docs/account-settings/account/account-deletion/delete-account-button.png)</Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the survey">
    Answer the survey, then click **Delete**.

    <Frame>![Account deletion survey dialog](/images/docs/account-settings/account/account-deletion/deletion-survey.png)</Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm identity">
    The deletion request is accepted only after identity verification:

    | Account authentication | How to confirm deletion |
    |------------------------|--------------------------|
    | 2FA enabled | Enter the one-time code from the authenticator app |
    | 2FA disabled, password account | Enter the account password |
    | 2FA disabled, OAuth / SSO account | Open the confirmation email and click the link |

    When the account uses 2FA or a password, successful verification activates the deletion request immediately. When the account uses OAuth or SSO and has no password, Gcore sends a confirmation email to the account email address, and the modal shows: "We've sent a confirmation link to your email, please check your inbox."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finish the email confirmation">
    This step applies only when the account uses OAuth or SSO and has no password. Open the link from the confirmation email:

    - A valid token activates the deletion request and opens the account settings page.
    - An expired, already used, or invalid token opens an error page. Return to account settings and submit a new request to receive a fresh confirmation link.
  </Step>
</Steps>

After the request is activated, the account is locked and services stay stopped unless the request is canceled within 10 days.

## Deletion restrictions

Unlike the procedure above, Gcore declines a deletion request when the account balance is negative, and the notification email explains the reason. Settle the outstanding balance, then submit a new request.
