title: Billing overview
sidebarTitle: Overview
description: Gcore billing overview explains monthly consumption collection and PAYG charges for Cloud, CDN, and Streaming.
---

Gcore bills its customers based on consumption data, collected on the 2nd day of each month to allow a 24-hour buffer for late-arriving usage records.

Cloud Pay-As-You-Go tariffs with threshold billing work differently. They are metered about every 5 minutes, and Gcore charges the account when costs add up to the 4 EUR or 4 USD threshold.

CDN Pay-As-You-Go charges the card when costs add up to the account credit limit.

Video Streaming Pay-As-You-Go charges the saved card when costs add up to the account credit limit. PAYG does not require a deposit or a minimum commitment. Sales can enable one monthly invoice for actual usage instead of those card charges.
