title: Billing overview
sidebarTitle: Overview
description: Gcore billing overview explains monthly consumption data collection and Pay-As-You-Go threshold billing charged at 4 EUR or 4 USD.
---

Gcore bills its customers based on consumption data, collected on the 2nd day of each month to allow a 24-hour buffer for late-arriving usage records.

Cloud Pay-As-You-Go tariffs with threshold billing work differently. They are metered about every 5 minutes, and Gcore charges the account when accrued costs reach the 4 EUR or 4 USD threshold.
