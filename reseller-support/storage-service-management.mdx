The Storage product management page in the Admin Portal controls a customer's product activation, trial period settings, and status.

Opening this page starts from the customer's account settings:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Accounts section">
  Find the customer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the customer's settings">
  Click the customer's ID, or click **Edit** in the Actions column.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Products tab">
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame>
![Edit account option in actions menu](https://assets.gcore.pro/docs/reseller-support/storage-service-management/edit-account.png)
</Frame>

## Activate product

Select the **Activate product** checkbox to let the customer use Storage.

<Frame>
![Activate product](https://assets.gcore.pro/docs/reseller-support/storage-service-management/storage-management-20.png)
</Frame>

<Note>
Clearing the **Activate product** checkbox may suspend active resources.
</Note>

Clearing the checkbox instead shows the customer an activation request form.

<Frame>
![Storage activation request form](https://assets.gcore.pro/docs/reseller-support/storage-service-management/storage-management-30.png)
</Frame>

## Trial settings

The **Trial settings** panel controls the trial period:

- The **Days limit** field sets how many days the trial lasts.
- The **Trial storage volume** field sets the storage limit during the trial, in GB.

The **Start day** and **End day** fields show the computed trial period and aren't editable directly.

<Frame>
![Trial settings](https://assets.gcore.pro/docs/reseller-support/storage-service-management/storage-management-40.png)
</Frame>

On the **Dashboard** page, customers see how many Storage trial days remain.

<Frame>
![Storage trial days remaining on the customer dashboard](https://assets.gcore.pro/docs/reseller-support/storage-service-management/storage-management-50.png)
</Frame>

## Transition to deleted status

The **Paused to Deleted** and **TrialEnd to Deleted** fields set how many days pass before the Storage product status switches to Deleted from each state; reaching Deleted status removes all of the customer's resources and files in Storage. The **Deleted status date** field shows when this happened and isn't editable directly.

<Frame>
![Storage product status timeline](https://assets.gcore.pro/docs/reseller-support/storage-service-management/storage-management-60.png)
</Frame>
