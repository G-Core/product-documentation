Settings on a FastEdge account set the network where that customer's applications are deployed and the plan that bills the usage.

In **General**, open **Accounts**, select the account ID, and on **Products** open **FastEdge setting** to open **Edit account**.

<Frame>
  ![FastEdge network and plan](/images/docs/reseller-support/fastedge/fastedge-management/edit-account.png)
</Frame>

## Networks

Applications are deployed on the selected network. **Gcore (default)** is the network selected for the account.

## Plan

**Plan** is the subscription plan the customer's usage is billed under. The list contains the plans available for that customer.

Click **Save changes** to apply the selected network and plan.

## Shared Access

In FastEdge, **Shared Access** lists the groups that decide which customers can use a shared template. A group shows its name, the accounts and templates assigned to it, and its description. **Create group** adds a group. Groups can also be managed with the [Groups&nbsp;API](/api-reference/fastedge-resellers/groups/list-groups).
