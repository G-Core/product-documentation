The **Templates** page lists the HTTP templates customers created in the Customer Portal. Customers in a group can create an application from a template shared with that group.

<Frame>![HTTP templates in FastEdge](/images/docs/reseller-support/fastedge/http-templates/templates.png)</Frame>

Narrow the list by:

- **Account ID** is the customer account where the template was created.
- **Group ID** is the [Shared&nbsp;Access](/reseller-support/fastedge/fastedge-management#shared-access) group the template is assigned to.

**Reset all** clears the filters. The current filters and columns can be saved as a preset.

**Name** and **Description** are the text the customer set in the Customer Portal. **Instructions**, in **Manage Columns**, are the usage notes supplied with the template. An empty **Group ID** means the template is not shared, so customers cannot open it or create an application from it.

To manage a template, select **View template** to open it, **Sharing** to assign it to a group or to clear that assignment, or **Delete template** to remove it from the Customer Portal. Sharing makes the template available to the customers in the group. Deleting a template is permanent, and restoring it requires uploading the template again.
