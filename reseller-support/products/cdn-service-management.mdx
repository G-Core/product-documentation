sidebarTitle: CDN management
title: Set CDN limits, features, and regions
description: Set how many CDN resources an account can have, which features it can use, and which regions deliver traffic.
---

Limits, features, and delivery regions are set on the CDN account. Turning a feature on lets the customer use it. A paid feature is charged on the customer's invoice.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to CDN > Accounts">
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Edit">
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Limits">
    **Maximum CDN resources limit** is how many CDN resources the account can have. **Maximum rules limit** is how many rules one resource can have. Zero in either field means no limit.

    <Frame>
      ![CDN account limits](/images/docs/reseller-support/products/cdn-service-management/limits.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Features">
    Free features:

    - **Enable gcdn.co zone (cname)**
    - **Show origin, shield and CDN traffic separately**
    - **Custom bd auth headers**
    - **Disable purge statuses**

    Paid features:

    - **Advanced directory statistics**
    - **advanced_user_agent_stat**
    - **Advanced geo countries statistics**
    - **Raw Logs (S3/FTP)**
    - **Origin Shielding**
    - **Resources with shared cache zone**
    - **Wildcard purge**
    - **Streaming**
    - **Image Stack**
    - **Prefetch**
    - **Custom SSL certificate**
    - **Logs uploader**

    <Frame>
      ![CDN account features](/images/docs/reseller-support/products/cdn-service-management/features.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Balancer">
    **Enable traffic balancer** makes **Content delivery regions** apply. Clear a region to leave it out of delivery. The regions are North America, Europe, CIS, Asia, Australia, Latin America, Middle East, and Africa. **Enable IPv6 delivery** includes IPv6.

    <Frame>
      ![CDN traffic balancer](/images/docs/reseller-support/products/cdn-service-management/balancer.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Save changes">
  </Step>
</Steps>
