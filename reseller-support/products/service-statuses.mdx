---
title: Set a product status
sidebarTitle: Product statuses
description: Set a product status on a customer account and the days before Deleted.
---

The account can see the product and cannot use it until the status is **Active**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to General > Identity and Access > Accounts">
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the account and select Products">
    <Frame>
      ![Products page on an account](/images/docs/reseller-support/products/service-statuses/products.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Show more">
    - While the status is **Trial** or **Active**, the product switch stays on. Set the status to **Trialend** or **Paused** before turning it off.
    - **Days limit**, **Start day**, and **End day** can be set when the status is **Trial**.
    - **Start day** stays fixed, and the switch beside **End day** makes that date editable.

    <Frame>
      ![Status and trial settings for a product](/images/docs/reseller-support/products/service-statuses/status.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the days before Deleted">
    - **Paused to Deleted** and **TrialEnd to Deleted** set how many days pass before the status becomes **Deleted**.
    - **Paused to Deleted** and **TrialEnd to Deleted** cannot be edited while the status is **New**.
    - **Deleted status date** cannot be edited.

    <Frame>
      ![Transition to deleted status](/images/docs/reseller-support/products/service-statuses/deleted-status.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>
