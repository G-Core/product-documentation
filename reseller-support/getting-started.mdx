Gcore sends a registration confirmation email once a reseller account is created. The confirmation link sets a password and stays active for 24 hours; if it expires or another issue comes up, contact [support@gcore.com](mailto:support@gcore.com). The Admin Portal is available at `https://admin.gcore.top/`.

## Account roles

The Admin Portal supports two account roles: Reseller and Seller.

### Reseller

The Reseller is the account admin, and only one user per organization holds this role. A Reseller can add Sellers, configure account branding, and generate statistics reports in the Admin Portal.

### Seller

A Seller is an additional user with access to the Admin Portal who can add clients and configure their resources; an organization can have any number of Sellers. Seller account creation and management is covered in [managing sellers](/reseller-support/manuals/manage-sellers).

## Customer Portal setup

Customers use the [Gcore Customer Portal](https://portal.gcore.com) to set up products, check statistics, and contact technical support, and it displays Gcore's corporate colors and logo by default unless custom branding is configured.

A reseller using its own Customer Portal and only needing the underlying product APIs can integrate directly with the [reseller API docs](/reseller-support/api) and the [product API docs](/api-reference/iam) without further Admin Portal configuration.

Branding the Customer Portal for customers involves configuring [product URLs](/reseller-support/manuals/set-up-control-panel-services-urls) and [vendor settings](/reseller-support/manuals/vendor-settings).

## Customer accounts

Logging in to the Admin Portal opens the **Accounts** section, where Sellers and the Reseller create customer accounts with **Add new account**; customers cannot self-register by default. Account creation and management is covered in [managing clients](/reseller-support/manuals/manage-clients).

Gcore also creates a customer account with the same email and company name as the Reseller account. This account collects customer statistics, generates [monthly reports](/reseller-support/manuals/generate-monthly-reports), and tracks Seller and Reseller accounts as its users, so it cannot be deleted.

Creating CDN resources for the Customer Portal products in this account is recommended, as covered when setting up product URLs.

## Product management

Gcore products available for reselling include CDN, WAAP, Streaming, Edge Cloud, Managed DNS, Storage, and FastEdge. Signing the contract includes discussing which products to resell with the account manager, and the selected products appear in the Customer Portal by default. Adjusting product visibility for all customers happens in the Admin Portal:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Vendor settings">
  Navigate to the **Vendor settings** section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Products page">
  Open the **Products** page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Adjust visibility">
  Enable or disable toggles next to each product to control its visibility in the Customer Portal.

  <Frame>![Vendor settings Products page with visibility toggles](https://assets.gcore.pro/docs/reseller-support/getting-started/vendor-settings-products.png)</Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Activating **Streaming** for customers gives each customer on the trial plan automatic access to one live stream.
</Note>

Overriding the **General settings** for an individual customer happens in that customer's settings:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the customer's account">
  In the sidebar, select **Accounts**. Find the customer and click its account ID to open the settings, or click the three-dot icon next to the customer and select **Edit**.

  <Frame><img src="https://assets.gcore.pro/docs/reseller-support/getting-started/edit-account.png" alt="Accounts list with the account ID and three-dot menu highlighted" width="70%"/></Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Adjust product access">
  On the **Products** page, select the products to activate or disable for the customer. A product with an **Active** or **Trial** status cannot be disabled directly; click **Show more** and change its status to **Trialend** or **Paused** first.

  <Frame>![Products page with activation and status controls](https://assets.gcore.pro/docs/reseller-support/getting-started/accounts-products.png)</Frame>

  Enabling a product displays it in the customer's Customer Portal, while disabling it removes it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Log in as the customer">
  Click **Log in** next to the three-dot icon to access the account as the customer's admin user.

  <Frame>![Log in as a user button next to the three-dot menu](https://assets.gcore.pro/docs/reseller-support/getting-started/log-in-as-a-user.png)</Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Displaying a product in the Customer Portal does not allow the customer to manage it by default; if the product is displayed but not configured, the customer sees a product activation request window instead.
</Note>

<Frame><img src="https://assets.gcore.pro/docs/reseller-support/getting-started/welcome-page-40.png" alt="Product activation request window shown to the customer" width="70%"/></Frame>

Submitting an activation request sends a notification email to either Gcore technical support or the reseller, depending on the **Support email** field in the **Branding settings** section.

The [Status Page](https://status.gcore.com) provides notifications about planned maintenance and incidents, with additional [subscription options](/account-settings/be-aware-of-the-service-status-scheduled-and-emergency-maintenance) beyond email.
