After account creation, an email arrives with a registration confirmation link. Set a password by following the link within 24 hours. Contact [support@gcore.com](mailto:support@gcore.com) if the link expires or any issues arise.

The Admin Portal is at `https://admin.gcore.top/`.

## Reseller and Seller roles

The Reseller role is the account administrator. Only one user in an organization can hold this role. A Reseller can add Sellers, configure account branding, and generate statistics reports in the Admin Portal.

## Inviting Sellers

A Seller is an additional Admin Portal user with the ability to add customers and configure their resources. There is no limit on the number of Sellers per organization. [Manage&nbsp;sellers](/reseller-support/manuals/manage-sellers) covers account creation and access configuration.

## Setting up the Customer Portal

Customers use the Customer Portal to configure products, check statistics, and contact technical support. Without additional configuration, customers see the Gcore Customer Portal at `https://portal.gcore.com` with Gcore branding.

## Creating customer accounts

After logging in to the Admin Portal, the browser opens the **Accounts** section. Customer accounts can be created by Sellers or the Reseller — customers cannot register directly. [Manage&nbsp;clients](/reseller-support/manuals/manage-clients) covers the full account management workflow.

When a Reseller account is created, Gcore also creates a companion customer account with the same email and company name. This account collects customer statistics, tracks [monthly&nbsp;reports](/reseller-support/manuals/generate-monthly-reports), and manages Seller and Reseller account access. Gcore recommends creating CDN resources for Customer Portal products in this account — [Portal&nbsp;URLs](/reseller-support/manuals/set-up-control-panel-services-urls) explains the setup. The companion account cannot be deleted.

## Product management

Available products for reselling include CDN, WAAP, Video Streaming, Edge Cloud, Managed DNS, Object Storage, FastEdge, and DDoS Protection. The exact products available depend on the reseller contract.

By default, the contracted products are visible in the Customer Portal. Global product visibility for all customers is managed in [Vendor&nbsp;settings](/reseller-support/manuals/vendor-settings).

To override product visibility for a specific customer:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Accounts section">
    In the sidebar, select **Accounts**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the customer account settings">
    Find the needed customer and click the account ID to open the settings. Alternatively, hover over the row and click the edit icon.

    <Frame>
    ![Accounts list with action icons visible on hover](/images/docs/reseller-support/getting-started/getting-started-image2.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Products tab">
    In the account settings, click the **Products** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure product visibility">
    Select or clear the checkboxes to activate or disable products for that customer.

    <Frame>
    ![Products tab showing available products with activation checkboxes](/images/docs/reseller-support/getting-started/getting-started-image3.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

To disable a product with **Active** or **Trial** status, click **Show more** and change the status to **Trialend** or **Paused** first.

Enabled products appear in the customer's Customer Portal. Disabled products are hidden.

To log in as the customer's admin user, open the account settings and click **Log in as Admin using account's credentials**.

<Frame>
![Account edit page with the Log in as Admin button](/images/docs/reseller-support/getting-started/getting-started-image4.png)
</Frame>

<Note>
Displaying a product in the Customer Portal does not grant the customer access by default. Until the product is configured, the customer sees a product activation request form. Activation requests are sent to Gcore support or to the address configured in the **Support email** field in [Vendor&nbsp;settings](/reseller-support/manuals/vendor-settings).
</Note>
