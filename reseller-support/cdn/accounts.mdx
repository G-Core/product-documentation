Connecting the CDN service to a customer account makes that account a CDN account. Each CDN resource belongs to that account and delivers content for a specific hostname. The content for a resource comes from an origin group.

<Frame>
![CDN accounts list](/images/docs/reseller-support/cdn/accounts/accounts.png)
</Frame>

## Managing CDN accounts and resources

Account-level settings control the CDN service status, resource limits, available features, and delivery regions. Set the status in [Product&nbsp;statuses](/reseller-support/accounts/service-statuses), and manage the limits, features, and regions in [CDN&nbsp;management](/reseller-support/cdn/cdn-service-management).

Resource-level settings control how CDN delivers content for an individual hostname. Manage each resource's availability in [CDN&nbsp;resources](/reseller-support/cdn/resources) and configure its content sources in [origin&nbsp;groups](/reseller-support/cdn/origin-groups). [Activity&nbsp;logs](/reseller-support/cdn/activity-logs) record resource and rule changes, as well as purge and prefetch requests.
