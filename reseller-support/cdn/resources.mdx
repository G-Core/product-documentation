---
title: Set content availability
sidebarTitle: CDN resources
description: Search the hostname list and control whether its content is available.
---

Each row is one hostname delivered through CDN, with its status, origin group, and resource type.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to CDN > CDN resources">
    - Search by CNAME or ID, or by account ID.
    - **CDN resource status** is **Active**, **Suspended**, **Processing**, or **Deleted**.
    - **Show only resources planned to automatic suspend** limits the list to resources scheduled for suspension.

    <Frame>
      ![Resources list](/images/docs/reseller-support/cdn/resources/resources.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the resource">
    Click the CNAME, or click the three dots at the end of the row and then **Settings**. **Edit account** and **Log in** are in that menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Content availability">
    On **Settings**, under **General**, **Content availability** controls delivery. While the switch is on, content is available to customers.

    [The&nbsp;same&nbsp;page](/reseller-support/cdn/resource-settings) sets the origin group, the certificate, and the cache. The options are grouped under **General**, **Cache**, **Access**, **Content**, **HTTP headers**, **Network limits**, **Optimization**, **Security**, and **FastEdge apps**.
  </Step>
</Steps>
