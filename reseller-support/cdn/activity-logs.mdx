Activity logs show changes to CDN resource and rule configuration, and purge and prefetch requests.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to CDN > Activity logs">
    Filter by **Requested at (UTC)**.

    - **Account ID** is the customer account.
    - **Method** and **Path** are the request.
    - **Status code** is the result.
    - **Data** shows the submitted change. A read request has no data.
  </Step>
</Steps>
