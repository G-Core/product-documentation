title: Streaming accounts
sidebarTitle: Accounts
description: Find a customer Streaming account, check its status, and open it.
---

Use the **Accounts** page to find a customer and check that customer's Streaming status. Deleted and test accounts are hidden by default. To find a specific customer, filter the list by **Account ID** or **Streaming status**.

<Frame>![Accounts in Streaming](/images/docs/reseller-support/streaming/accounts/accounts.png)</Frame>

After the account is found, select **Log in** to manage that customer's videos, or **Edit** to control which [Streaming&nbsp;features](/reseller-support/streaming/management) are available to that account.
