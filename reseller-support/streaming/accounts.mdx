**Accounts** is where a reseller finds a customer and checks that customer's Streaming status.

<Frame>![Accounts in Streaming](/images/docs/reseller-support/streaming/accounts/accounts.png)</Frame>

**Hide deleted accounts** and **Hide test accounts** are selected, so those rows stay out of the list. **Account ID** narrows the list to one account. **Streaming status** filters the list by the status shown on the row.

A row shows the account ID, the email, the company name, the creation time, and the Streaming status. **Log in** opens that customer's Video Hosting. **Edit** opens [Edit&nbsp;account](/reseller-support/products/streaming) to review the trial limits and change the feature switches.
