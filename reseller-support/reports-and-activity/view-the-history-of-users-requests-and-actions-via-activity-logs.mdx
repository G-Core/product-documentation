Activity logs show requests and sign-in attempts.

Navigate to **General** > **Activity logs** > **Platform** for requests. Each row shows who sent the request, when, which call, and the status code.

Navigate to **General** > **Activity logs** > **Logins** for sign-in attempts. The date range starts at **Last 24 hours**. Set **Success** to **false** to keep failed attempts.
