---
title: Account deletion and failed sign-in notices
sidebarTitle: Email alerts
description: Configure email notifications for account deletion events and failed admin sign-ins.
---

Your team is notified when an account deletion is requested, canceled, or completed, and when an admin portal sign-in fails.

Navigate to **General** > **Vendor settings** > **Email alerts**.

<Frame>
![Email alerts for deletion requests and failed sign-ins](/images/docs/reseller-support/customer-portal/email-alerts/email-alerts.png)
</Frame>

Enable **Email for deletion requests** and enter the email address that should receive notifications when an account deletion is requested, canceled, or completed.

Enable **Email for unsuccessful login attempts** and enter the email address for notifications about failed admin portal sign-ins.

Click **Save**.
