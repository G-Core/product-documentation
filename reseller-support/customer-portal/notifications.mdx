title: Email sent to end users
sidebarTitle: Notifications

---

These switches control email sent to end users. Deletion and failed sign-in notices for the reseller are set in [Email&nbsp;alerts](/reseller-support/customer-portal/email-alerts).

Navigate to **General** > **Vendor settings** > **Notifications**.

<Frame>
![Notification groups for end-user email](/images/docs/reseller-support/customer-portal/notifications/notifications.png)
</Frame>

Notices are grouped by product. Turn a product group on to allow its email, then clear any notice inside the group that end users should not receive. Keep **Real-time incidents** and **Scheduled maintenance** on. The page recommends those two.
