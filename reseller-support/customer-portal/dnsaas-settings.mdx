---
title: Contact and name servers for Managed DNS
sidebarTitle: DNSaaS settings
description: Set the SOA contact email and the name servers customers delegate to.
---

These fields set the contact email and the name servers that customers of Managed DNS see. Until the fields are set, customers see the Gcore contact email and name servers.

Navigate to **General** > **Vendor settings** > **DNSaaS settings**.

<Frame>
![DNS Settings with a contact address and two name servers](/images/docs/reseller-support/customer-portal/dnsaas-settings/dns-settings.png)
</Frame>

**Contact** is the email address used in the SOA record. **Primary name server** and **Secondary name server** are the hostnames customers delegate a domain to. Enter the hostnames for this brand.

Click **Save**.

<Info>
A domain that is not yet delegated lists these hostnames as the name servers to set.
</Info>
