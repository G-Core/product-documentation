---
title: Contact and name servers for Managed DNS
sidebarTitle: DNSaaS settings
description: Set the SOA contact email and the name servers customers delegate to.
---

Configure the contact email and name servers shown to Managed DNS customers. Until these settings are changed, customers see the Gcore contact email and name servers.

Navigate to **General** > **Vendor settings** > **DNSaaS settings**.

<Frame>
![DNS Settings with a contact address and two name servers](/images/docs/reseller-support/customer-portal/dnsaas-settings/dns-settings.png)
</Frame>

Set **Contact** to the email address used in the SOA record. Enter the name server hostnames for this brand in **Primary name server** and **Secondary name server**.

Click **Save**.

<Info>
For domains that have not yet been delegated, customers see these hostnames as the name servers to configure.
</Info>
