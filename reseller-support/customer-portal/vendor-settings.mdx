Branding settings let you customize the Customer Portal with your company's identity, including its domain, logos, and colors. You can also configure the support contacts and resources available to your customers.

Navigate to **General** > **Vendor settings** > **Branding settings**.

![Vendor settings menu with Branding settings selected](/images/docs/reseller-support/customer-portal/vendor-settings/branding-settings.png)
</Frame>

## Set up the branded domain

Customers access the sign-in page, Customer Portal, and Gcore products through your company's domain.

Contact your account manager and provide the domain you want to use for your customer portal. [Create&nbsp;the&nbsp;DNS&nbsp;records](/dns/dns-records/manage-dns-records-non-advanced-interface-mode) the account manager specifies. For `example.com`, those records use the hosts below. [Product&nbsp;URLs](/reseller-support/customer-portal/set-up-control-panel-services-urls) lists the hosts of the other products.

| Service | Host |
| --- | --- |
| Sign-in | `auth.example.com` |
| Customer portal | `portal.example.com` |
| API | `api.example.com` |

Gcore then completes the setup and sets **Base customer portal domain** to that domain.

<Caution>
Do not change **Base customer portal domain** manually.
</Caution>

On **Branding settings**, read **Base customer portal domain**. The field shows the domain you sent to the account manager after Gcore writes it there. That is the sign that this part of the setup is finished.

## Customize the portal appearance

Enter the company name in **Brand name**. It appears on the welcome page, in the browser tab, as the sender name in emails, and in the **Website** item of the **Help** menu.

Upload your logos and favicon using the following fields:

| Field | Where it appears | Requirements |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Logo at the sign-in page** | Customer sign-in page | SVG (preferred), PNG, or JPG; up to 4 MB; 300 x 90 px |
| **Header logo** | Customer portal menu and email | SVG (preferred), PNG, or JPG; up to 4 MB; 75 x 40 px |
| **Favicon** | Browser tab | ICO; 16 x 16 px |

Select a **Brand color** for the **Log in** button, selected menu items, and links and product icons on hover.

Click **Save**.

## Configure customer support and Help links

Enter **Support phone number** and **Support email** to add your contact details to the **Help** menu.

To add links, enable the corresponding options and enter their URLs. **Link to agreement** appears as **Legal documents**, and **Link to website** appears as **Website**. Links without a URL or with their option disabled are hidden.

Click **Save**.

![Help link options for agreement, website, API documentation, product documentation, and the status page](/images/docs/reseller-support/customer-portal/vendor-settings/help-links.png)

## Configure customer account behavior

Enable **Show Create a new account** to allow customers to create multiple accounts with the same email address and switch between them in **Profile**. Otherwise, customers can register only one account per email.

Enable **Portal Mode** to redirect customers to the portal domain after signing in.

Under **Languages**, select which of the available languages — English, German, Russian, and Chinese — your customers can use.

Click **Save**.
