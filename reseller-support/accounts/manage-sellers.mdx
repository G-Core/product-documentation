---
title: Manage sellers
description: Invite a seller, update the seller, sign in as that seller, and delete the seller.
---

A seller accepts the invitation before signing in from the sign-in page.

## Add a seller

The email and the name go out with the invitation. The phone can stay empty.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Sellers">
    Navigate to **General** > **Identity and Access** > **Sellers** and click **Create Seller**.

    <Frame>
    ![Sellers list with two sellers and Create Seller](/images/docs/reseller-support/accounts/manage-sellers/manage-sellers-image1.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Seller details">
    Enter the email and the seller name. **Phone (Optional)** can stay empty.

    <Frame>
    ![Create Seller dialog with email, seller name, and phone](/images/docs/reseller-support/accounts/manage-sellers/manage-sellers-image2.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the invitation">
    Click **Send invite**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The seller appears in the list with status **Not activated**. The invite date appears below the status. **Blocked** reads **false**. The seller creates a password from the invitation and finishes registration. This page does not set the password.

## Change the seller

Open the three-dot menu on the row.

<Frame>
![Seller row menu with Edit, Resend invite, Block, and Delete](/images/docs/reseller-support/accounts/manage-sellers/manage-sellers-image3.png)
</Frame>

**Resend invite** sends the invitation again while the status is **Not activated**.

**Edit** changes the email, the seller name, and the phone. Click **Submit** to save the changes.

<Frame>
![Edit seller dialog with email, seller name, and phone](/images/docs/reseller-support/accounts/manage-sellers/manage-sellers-image4.png)
</Frame>

**Block** is covered in [Block&nbsp;or&nbsp;activate](/reseller-support/accounts/block-activate-users-and-sellers).

## Sign in as the seller

In **Actions**, rest on the arrow. It is labeled **Log in**. That opens the Admin Portal as that seller, before the seller has accepted the invitation. No password is required.

<Frame>
![Sellers row with the Log in label on the arrow](/images/docs/reseller-support/accounts/manage-sellers/manage-sellers-image6.png)
</Frame>

On the sign-in page, **Forgot password?** sends a reset link to the email entered there. The row menu does not contain **Reset password** while the seller is **Not activated**.

## Delete a seller

In the row menu, click **Delete**.

<Frame>
![Delete confirmation for a seller](/images/docs/reseller-support/accounts/manage-sellers/manage-sellers-image5.png)
</Frame>

<Warning>
Clients assigned to this seller are left without a seller.
</Warning>

Click **Delete**. The seller is removed from the list.
