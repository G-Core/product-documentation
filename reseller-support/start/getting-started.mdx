---
title: Getting started
description: Create customer accounts and set which products those accounts can see.
---

Customer access in the reseller portal depends on two things: customer accounts and product visibility. Global visibility provides the default product set, while personal visibility handles exceptions for individual accounts.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Confirm the registration email">
    Gcore sends a registration confirmation email once a reseller account is created. The confirmation link sets a password and stays active for 24 hours. If the link expires, contact [support@gcore.com](mailto:support@gcore.com). The Admin Portal is available at `https://admin.gcore.top/`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create customer accounts">
    Navigate to **General** > **Identity and Access** > **Accounts**. [Manage&nbsp;accounts](/reseller-support/accounts/manage-clients) covers adding an account, signing in as that customer, and deletion.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the default product visibility">
    A new account shows the products enabled for every customer. [Global&nbsp;visibility](/reseller-support/product-visibility/configure-global-visibility) sets that default.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Override visibility for one account">
    [Personal&nbsp;visibility](/reseller-support/product-visibility/configure-personal-visibility) overrides that default for one account. When a product is enabled for the account, its status starts at **New**, so the customer can see it but cannot use it yet.
  </Step>
</Steps>
