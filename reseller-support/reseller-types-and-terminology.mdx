Definitions for terminology used throughout Gcore reseller documentation.

**Bill-to-Party reseller** is a reseller type where usage and billing are tracked per customer, and each customer can have individual pricing; despite this per-customer tracking, Gcore still combines every customer's usage into one invoice billed to the reseller.

**Classic reseller** is a reseller type where every customer shares the reseller's default pricing and can't view billing details, and all customer usage combines into a single invoice for the reseller.

**Customer** is an account created by a reseller or seller in the [Accounts section](/reseller-support/manuals/manage-clients); customers can't self-register. Gcore's broader account terminology calls this a sub-customer, a different concept from a sub-account, which is an additional account under the same login rather than a separate reseller-managed customer.

**Reseller** is a Gcore partner that resells products to its own customers under its own brand, with access to the Admin Portal to manage those customers, their services, and [branding settings](/reseller-support/manuals/vendor-settings). A reseller offering full branding customization, including a custom domain and landing page, is known as a white-label reseller.

**Seller** is a user a reseller adds to help manage customers, created on the [Sellers tab](/reseller-support/manuals/manage-sellers); a seller can create, edit, and manage customer accounts and their services.

**Technical account** is the account automatically created for every reseller, using the reseller's own email address and company name. It collects usage statistics for [monthly reports](/reseller-support/manuals/generate-monthly-reports) and lists every user in the reseller's Admin Portal; it can't be deleted.
