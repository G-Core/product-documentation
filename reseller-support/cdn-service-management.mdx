Configuring a customer's CDN service in the Admin Portal covers activating the product, controlling paid and free features, and setting traffic balancing regions.

## General CDN settings

Opening a customer's CDN settings starts from the account settings:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Accounts section">
  Find the customer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the customer's settings">
  Click the customer's ID, or click the three-dot icon and select **Edit**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame>
![Customer account settings](https://assets.gcore.pro/docs/reseller-support/cdn-service-management/cdn-settings-10.png)
</Frame>

## Manage general settings

The customer's settings page covers the **Products** and **Features** tabs.

<Frame>
![Services](https://assets.gcore.pro/docs/reseller-support/cdn-service-management/cdn-settings-20.png)
</Frame>

### Activate CDN

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Products page">
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the product settings">
  Click **Show more**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Activate the product">
  Select the **Activate product** checkbox to let the customer create and manage CDN resources; clearing it instead shows the customer a product activation request form on the CDN tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save changes">
  Click **Save changes** to apply.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Clearing the **Activate product** checkbox may suspend active resources.
</Note>

### Transition to deleted status

The **Paused to Deleted** and **TrialEnd to Deleted** fields on the Products page set how many days pass before the CDN product status switches to Deleted from each state; reaching Deleted status deletes the customer's CDN resources. The **Deleted status date** field shows when this happened and isn't editable directly.

## Free and paid features

Click **CDN settings** to see the list of available features.

<Frame>
![Services](https://assets.gcore.pro/docs/reseller-support/cdn-service-management/cdn-settings-30.png)
</Frame>

The CDN application provides an alternate path to the same features:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the customer list">
  In the **CDN** section, open the customer list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the customer's settings">
  Click the customer's ID, or click the three-dot icon and select **Edit**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Features tab">
  Navigate to the **Features** tab.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame>
![Edit customer](https://assets.gcore.pro/docs/reseller-support/cdn-service-management/cdn-settings-40-v2.png)
</Frame>

Available features appear as a list with toggle buttons, some free and some paid. The free **Show Origin, Shield, and CDN Traffic Separately** option splits Customer Portal traffic reports into separate origin, shield, and CDN figures instead of one combined report; it's disabled by default, so traffic data appears combined until enabled.

Paid features include [usage statistics](/cdn/view-statistics-of-a-cdn-resource) broken down by directory, user agent, and geography, [raw logs](/cdn/logs/raw-logs-export-cdn-resource-logs-to-your-storage), [origin shielding](/cdn/cdn-resource-options/general/enable-and-configure-origin-shielding), and [Video Streaming](/cdn/video-cdn/hls); setting shielding locations requires contacting technical support or the account manager.

<Frame>
![Paid features](https://assets.gcore.pro/docs/reseller-support/cdn-service-management/cdn-settings-50.png)
</Frame>

Click **Save changes** after enabling a feature. Shared cache zone, wildcard purge, and Image Stack features require contacting the Gcore support team at [support@gcore.com](mailto:support@gcore.com) to enable; additional detail for each feature is available by hovering over the question-mark icon next to its name.

## Balancer

Geobalancer groups CDN servers into North America, CIS, Australia, Middle East, Africa, Europe, Asia, Latin America, and World. Selecting World uses anycast addressing, so content is served from whichever region delivers it most efficiently at that moment.

<Frame>
![Balancer tab](https://assets.gcore.pro/docs/reseller-support/cdn-service-management/balancer.png)
</Frame>

### Set traffic balancer

Traffic balancer routing is disabled by default. Enabling it allows regions to be excluded from delivery when no end users are located there:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enable the balancer">
  Click **Enable traffic balancer**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select IP delivery">
  Check the **Enable IPv6 delivery** checkbox to include IPv6 addresses in responses (AAAA record), or clear it to use only IPv4 addresses (A record).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Exclude regions">
  Clear the checkboxes for regions to exclude from delivery.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save changes">
  Click **Save changes**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Caution>
Disabling regions for customers also requires disabling the World region, or the balancer won't work correctly.
</Caution>

![Balancer settings](https://assets.gcore.pro/docs/reseller-support/cdn-service-management/balancer-set.png)
</Frame>
