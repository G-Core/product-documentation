DNS becomes available to a reseller's customers through the same [global visibility](/reseller-support/manuals/configure-global-visibility) and [personal visibility](/reseller-support/manuals/configure-personal-visibility) settings used for every other product, though unlike CDN, Cloud, Object Storage, and WAAP, DNS has no per-account **Activate product**, trial, or deletion transition controls on the **Products** tab, so visibility settings are the only account-level control available for it. Once visible, the **DNSaaS settings** section lets resellers replace Gcore's own name servers and SOA contact with their own.

## Configure DNS branding

Navigate to **Vendor settings** > **DNSaaS settings** to white-label the DNS service shown to customers.

<Frame>
![DNSaaS settings with primary name server, secondary name server, and SOA email fields](https://assets.gcore.pro/docs/reseller-support/manuals/vendor-settings/dnsaas-settings-190.png)
</Frame>

All three fields default to Gcore's own values until a reseller sets custom ones.

The **Primary name server** and **Secondary name server** fields replace Gcore's own name servers wherever a customer's Customer Portal displays them for a DNS zone. The **SOA email** field replaces the email address used in that zone's SOA (Start of Authority) record.
