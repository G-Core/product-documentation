---
title: Plans on customer accounts
sidebarTitle: Addendums
description: See the plan, product, and active dates on a customer account.
---

Each row is a plan on a customer account, with the product, the currency, and the dates the plan is active. Status is **Upcoming**, **Active**, **Stopped**, or **Paused**.

Navigate to **General** > **Billing** > **Addendums**.

**Plan name** opens the plan. Hover that name for the plan ID used by the **Expenses** template on [Reports](/reseller-support/reports-and-activity/generate-monthly-reports).
