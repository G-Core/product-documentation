---
title: Invoices and credit notes
sidebarTitle: ERP Invoices
description: Download an invoice or a credit note as a PDF.
---

Each row is an invoice or a credit note for a customer account. **Type** is **Invoice** or **Credit note**, and **Status** is **Not paid**, **Paid**, **Partial**, **In payment**, or **Reversed**.

Navigate to **General** > **Billing** > **ERP Invoices**.

**Download PDF** on the row saves the file. When the file is not ready, the page reports that the invoice PDF is temporarily unavailable.
