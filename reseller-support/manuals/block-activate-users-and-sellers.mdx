---
title: Block or activate a seller
ai-navigation: Describe how resellers block, activate, and check the block status of a seller account.
---

Blocking a seller revokes their access to the Admin Portal without deleting the account; while blocked, a seller can't sign in, recover a password, or use an activation link. Activating a blocked seller reverses this and restores login access.

## Block or activate a seller

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the seller's menu">
  1. Navigate to the **Sellers** tab of the **Users** section.
  2. Find the seller using the filters.
  3. Click the **...** icon in the **Actions** column.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change the seller's status">
  Select **Block** to revoke the seller's access, or **Activate** to restore access to a seller who is already blocked.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
While blocked, all of a seller's requests to the Admin Portal API are denied, including authorization, password recovery, and activation requests.
</Note>

## Check whether a seller is blocked

Navigate to the **Sellers** tab of the **Users** section and check the seller's **Blocked** column: `true` means the seller is blocked and has no access to the account or its products, and `false` means the seller is active.
