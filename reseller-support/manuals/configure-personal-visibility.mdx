The account's **Products** tab overrides the [global visibility](/reseller-support/manuals/configure-global-visibility) settings for one customer, controlling which products appear in that customer's Customer Portal regardless of the reseller-wide default.

</Frame>

## Enable or disable a product

Toggling a product on makes that product's tab appear in the account's Customer Portal, but doesn't activate it; activating a product is a separate step covered in [CDN service management](/reseller-support/cdn-service-management).

Toggling a product off only succeeds if the account's status for that product isn't Trial or Active, because a product can't be disabled while a customer is actively using it; the account's status must first move to Trialend or Paused before the toggle can turn the product off.

<Frame>
<img src="/images/docs/reseller-support/manuals/configure-personal-visibility/configure-personal-visibility-image1.png" alt="Error message when disabling a product that is in trial or active status" width="70%"/>
</Frame>

Disabling a product for an account deactivates all of that account's settings for the product and removes it from the customer's Customer Portal.

## Interaction with global visibility settings

Once a product's visibility is set for a specific account, later changes to the global visibility settings no longer affect that account; the personal setting takes priority and stays in effect until it's changed again.
