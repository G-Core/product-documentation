The **Products** tab of [Vendor settings](/reseller-support/manuals/vendor-settings) sets which products are available to every customer account by default, unless a customer has a [personal visibility](/reseller-support/manuals/configure-personal-visibility) setting that overrides it.

## Enable or disable a product

Toggling a product on activates it for every customer who doesn't have a personal override for that product.

Toggling a product off only succeeds if none of the affected customers have that product in the Trial or Active status, because a product can't be disabled while a customer is actively using it; those customers must first move to the Trialend or Paused status before the global toggle can turn the product off. This is the same status restriction that applies when disabling a product for one account.

For a customer without a personal override, disabling a product globally:

- Deactivates the product in that customer's personal visibility settings.
- Deactivates all of that customer's settings for the product, found on the account's **Products** tab (**Accounts** > account ID > **Products**).
- Removes the product from that customer's Customer Portal.

A newly created customer account has every product from the global visibility settings available by default.
