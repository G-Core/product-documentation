The **Vendor settings** section configures a reseller's branding, customer notification emails, SMTP and DNS settings, product visibility, and notification categories, split across six tabs: Branding settings, Email alerts, SMTP Provider Settings, DNSaaS settings, Products, and Notifications.

## Branding settings

Branding settings replace Gcore's default logo, colors, and contact information with the reseller's own in the customer's Customer Portal; by default, before branding is configured, the Customer Portal shows Gcore's own links, email address, and phone number.

### Domain and portal mode

The **Base customer portal domain** field sets the domain used to generate [product URLs](/reseller-support/manuals/set-up-control-panel-services-urls) for customers: entering `company.com` makes the sign-in page `auth.company.com`, the CDN page `cdn.company.com`, and the Cloud page `cloud.company.com`.

<Frame>
![Base customer portal domain field on the Branding settings page](/images/docs/reseller-support/manuals/vendor-settings/vendor-settings-image1.png)
</Frame>

<Caution>
Changing this field after the Customer Portal is in active use can cause issues; avoid changing it once set.
</Caution>

The **Enable Portal Mode** toggle redirects all of a reseller's customers to the configured domain after sign-in.

### Colors and logo

The **Brand name** field sets the name displayed in the browser tab title, the **Help** menu's **Website** item, the Welcome page, and in email notifications and their sender name. The **Languages** setting controls which languages are available to customers in the Customer Portal.

The **Logo at the sign-in page** field sets the logo shown on the sign-in page, limited to 4 MB and a 300 x 90 px resolution, in SVG (recommended), PNG, or JPG format.

The **Header logo** field sets a smaller logo shown in the product column of the Customer Portal and in emails, limited to 4 MB and a 75 x 40 px resolution, in SVG (recommended), PNG, or JPG format.

The **Favicon** field sets the site favicon, which must use the ICO format at a 16 x 16 px resolution.

The **Brand color** field highlights the selected menu item and colors links and product icons in the navigation menu on hover.

### Customer support tools

The **Help** menu in the customer's Customer Portal shows contact details and links configured on this tab, including the technical support phone number and email, links to legal documents, API documentation, product documentation, the Status Page, and the Roadmap; a link that isn't configured doesn't appear in the **Help** menu.

The **Support phone number** field sets the technical support phone number shown to customers, and the **Support email** field sets the technical support email address shown to customers.

<Frame>
![Support email](https://assets.gcore.pro/docs/reseller-support/manuals/vendor-settings/contact-reseller.png)
</Frame>

The **Link to agreement** field sets the link shown as **Legal documents** in the **Help** menu, and the **Link to website** field sets the link shown as **Website**.

The **Show API Documentation** toggle sets the link shown as **API Documentation** in the **Help** menu, and the **Show Product Documentation** toggle sets the link shown as **Product Documentation**.

The **Show Status Page** toggle sets the link shown as **Status Page** in the **Help** menu, and the **Show Roadmap** toggle sets the link shown as **Roadmap**.

<Frame>
![Useful links for customers](https://assets.gcore.pro/docs/reseller-support/manuals/vendor-settings/links-info.png)
</Frame>

The **Show Create a new account** toggle allows customers to create additional accounts with the same email and switch between them from the **Profile** menu; when disabled, a customer can register only one account per email.

## Email alerts

The **Email alerts** tab sets email addresses that receive notifications about customer account deletion requests and failed login attempts, separately from the notifications customers themselves receive.

The **Email for deletion requests** field receives notifications when a customer requests account deletion, cancels a deletion request, or has an account deleted. The **Email for unsuccessful login attempts** field receives notifications about unsuccessful login attempts across all admin portal users, which helps identify suspicious account activity.

Enabling either alert requires turning on its toggle and entering an email address; clicking the **+** button adds additional addresses, and changes take effect after clicking **Save**.

![Email alerts settings with deletion request and login attempt toggles](/images/docs/reseller-support/manuals/vendor-settings/vendor-settings-image3.png)

## SMTP Provider Settings

By default, customers receive automatic product emails, including trial start and end notices, password changes, and new user additions, from a Gcore mail provider using the address `support@gcore.com`. Sending these emails from a reseller's own address instead requires setting up an SMTP server:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set the base domain">
  Open **Branding settings** and verify the **Base customer portal domain** field is filled in; if it's empty, emails aren't sent.

  <Frame>
  <img src="/images/docs/reseller-support/manuals/vendor-settings/vendor-settings-image1.png" alt="Branding settings page with the base customer portal domain filled in" width="70%"/>
  </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable product emails">
  Open the **SMTP Provider Settings** tab and select the **Email for product emails** checkbox.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure the SMTP server">
  Enter the **Email from** address that appears in the **From** field of outgoing emails, the SMTP **Server address** (a domain like `smtp.serveraddress.com`, or an IP address), and the **Port** the server uses, commonly 25 or 587. Select **TLS** if the server uses secure sockets, then enter the server's **Username** and **Password**.

  <Frame>
  <img src="/images/docs/reseller-support/manuals/vendor-settings/vendor-settings-image2.png" alt="SMTP Provider Settings form with product emails enabled" width="70%"/>
  </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and test">
  Click **Save**, then click **Send test mail** to confirm the configuration works; the test email goes to the account's primary email address.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## DNSaaS settings

Resellers offering the Managed DNS product configure the **Contact** email address, **Primary name server**, and **Secondary name server** used to generate SOA records; these values appear in the customer's Customer Portal when adding a domain to a DNS zone. Before configuration, customers see Gcore's own primary and secondary name servers and Gcore's email address.

## Products

The **Products** tab controls which products are available and visible to customers, at two levels of visibility: global and personal. The [global visibility](/reseller-support/manuals/configure-global-visibility) settings set product visibility for all customers by default.

<Frame>
![Global products in vendor settings](https://assets.gcore.pro/docs/reseller-support/manuals/vendor-settings/vendor-settings-products.png)
</Frame>

The [personal visibility](/reseller-support/manuals/configure-personal-visibility) settings enable or disable a product for one customer; once a product's visibility is set personally, changes to the global visibility settings no longer affect that customer.

<Tip>
Activating the Video Streaming product for customers gives each customer on the trial plan automatic access to one live stream.
</Tip>

<Frame>
![List of account products](https://assets.gcore.pro/docs/reseller-support/manuals/vendor-settings/vendor-settings-product-visibility.png)
</Frame>

## Notifications

The **Notifications** tab controls which categories of email notifications each Gcore product can send to customers, grouped by product. Each group has a master toggle plus individual toggles for specific notification types within it; disabling a group's master toggle disables all of its notification types for customers.

- **System notifications**: account-level notifications, including reminders during the base trial and updates about account deletion requests.
- **General notifications**: cross-product notifications, including payments, product trial start and end, expiring API tokens, and Protected Network alerts.
- **CDN notifications**: SSL certificate errors and expiration, resource suspension, and traffic consumption limits.
- **Object Storage notifications**: bucket object count and storage request limits.
- **Cloud notifications**: DDoS attack alerts, Kubernetes cluster certificate renewal, resource cleanup, and AI cluster creation.
- **Streaming notifications**: live stream start and stop alerts.
- **Colocation notifications**: scheduled maintenance alerts.
