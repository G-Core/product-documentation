Customer accounts are created in the [Admin&nbsp;Portal](https://admin.gcore.top/general/accounts). Each account is how one customer signs in and sees the products made available to them. The [reseller&nbsp;discount](/hosting/become-reseller-and-resell-our-services) is a different program, a discount on Virtual Server and Dedicated Server purchases, without this portal.

## Start

A reseller account exists before the first sign-in. [Become&nbsp;a&nbsp;reseller](/reseller-support/start/become-a-gcore-reseller) covers issuance of that account. [Getting&nbsp;started](/reseller-support/start/getting-started) follows the first sign-in through a customer account and the products on it.

## Accounts

[Manage&nbsp;accounts](/reseller-support/accounts/manage-clients) adds a customer account, edits it, and requests deletion. [Manage&nbsp;sellers](/reseller-support/accounts/manage-sellers) adds people who can create those accounts. [Block&nbsp;or&nbsp;activate](/reseller-support/accounts/block-activate-users-and-sellers) removes a seller's access without deleting the seller.

## Product visibility

A new account shows the products enabled for every customer. [Global&nbsp;visibility](/reseller-support/product-visibility/configure-global-visibility) sets that default. [Personal&nbsp;visibility](/reseller-support/product-visibility/configure-personal-visibility) overrides it on one account. A product left on appears with status **New**, so the customer sees it and cannot use it yet.

## Products

Use of a product starts after it is visible, and the screen depends on the product: [CDN](/reseller-support/products/cdn-service-management), [Storage](/reseller-support/products/storage-service-management), [Cloud](/reseller-support/products/cloud-service-management), [FastEdge](/reseller-support/products/fastedge-management), and [Streaming](/reseller-support/products/streaming). [Product&nbsp;statuses](/reseller-support/products/service-statuses) are the states a product moves through on the account.

## Security

[API&nbsp;tokens](/reseller-support/security/api-tokens) let an application make requests for the account. [Password&nbsp;policy](/reseller-support/security/password-policy) sets how often passwords expire. [Authorization](/reseller-support/security/authorization) connects a SAML identity provider.

## Customer portal

Brand, address, and sign-in belong to the portal the customer opens. [Vendor&nbsp;settings](/reseller-support/customer-portal/vendor-settings) cover branding and customer email. [Product&nbsp;URLs](/reseller-support/customer-portal/set-up-control-panel-services-urls) set the portal address.

## Reports and activity

[Monthly&nbsp;reports](/reseller-support/reports-and-activity/generate-monthly-reports) summarize usage across customer accounts. [Activity&nbsp;logs](/reseller-support/reports-and-activity/view-the-history-of-users-requests-and-actions-via-activity-logs) record requests and sign-ins.

## API

The [reseller&nbsp;API](/reseller-support/api) performs the same account, branding, and product operations. [API&nbsp;tokens](/reseller-support/security/api-tokens) is the credential those requests send.
