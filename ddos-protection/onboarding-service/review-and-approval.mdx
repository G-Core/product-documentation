After [network submission](/ddos-protection/onboarding-service/network-submission), the network operations center (NOC) team reviews the request and approves, rejects, or revokes it. A decision often arrives by email within 24–48 hours, though that interval is not guaranteed.

## Review process

The NOC team follows three steps to reach a decision.

1. The team receives the request and adds it to the review queue.
2. The team checks the network CIDR format, AS number validity, and Letter of Authorization (LOA) document, verifying that all information matches the submission.
3. The team approves, rejects, or revokes the submission. Approval and rejection both trigger an email notification; a rejection email includes the additional information required. A revocation resets the submission to Pending status, removes the recorded AS number and prefixes, and includes a NOC comment explaining the reason.

## Email notification

An approval email includes the following details:

- Confirmation of approval.
- Approved AS number.
- Approved network (CIDR).
- Instructions for the next steps.

A rejection email lists what to correct. Update the CIDR, AS number, or LOA as instructed, then submit the network again.

A revocation email notifies you that the submission has been reset to Pending status. The recorded AS number and prefixes are removed. Review the NOC comment in the email, correct the submission, and resubmit the network.

## Waiting for approval

While waiting for a decision:

- Watch for the approval or rejection notification by email, including the spam folder.
- Prepare the GRE endpoint IP of the local router.

Once the network is approved, continue with [network configuration](/ddos-protection/onboarding-service/network-configuration) to apply the protection settings.
