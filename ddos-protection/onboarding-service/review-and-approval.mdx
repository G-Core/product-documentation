After [network submission](/ddos-protection/onboarding-service/network-submission), the network operations center (NOC) team reviews the request and either approves or rejects it.

## Review process

The NOC team follows three steps to reach a decision.

1. The team receives the request and adds it to the review queue.
2. The team checks the network CIDR format, AS number validity, and Letter of Authorization (LOA) document, verifying that all information matches the submission.
3. The team approves or rejects the request. Approval and rejection both trigger an email notification; a rejection email includes the additional information required.

## Email notification

An approval email includes the following details:

- Confirmation of approval.
- Approved AS number.
- Approved network (CIDR).
- Instructions for the next steps.

## Waiting for approval

While waiting for a decision:

- Watch for the approval notification by email.
- Confirm the [Gcore Customer Portal](https://portal.gcore.com) is accessible.
- Prepare the GRE endpoint information, if available.

Once the network is approved, continue with [network configuration](/ddos-protection/onboarding-service/network-configuration) to apply the protection settings.
