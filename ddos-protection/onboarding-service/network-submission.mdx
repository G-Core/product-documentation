Network submission is the second step in the [setup process](/ddos-protection/onboarding-service/service-activation), after [plan activation](/ddos-protection/onboarding-service/plan-activation). Submit the network to protect by providing its network details and a Letter of Authorization (LOA) document.

## Submission steps

Complete the following steps in the [Gcore Customer Portal](https://portal.gcore.com) to submit a network for protection.

1. Access the **Protected networks** page.
   - In the **DDoS Protection** dashboard, click **Add protected network**.
   - This opens the network submission form.

   <Frame>
     ![Protected network form](/images/docs/ddos-protection/onboarding-service/custom-protection-profile-configuration/protected-networks.png)
   </Frame>
2. Complete the required fields.
   - **Network (CIDR format)**
     - Enter the network in CIDR notation (for example, `198.51.100.0/24`).
     - Minimum prefix length: `/24`.
     - Examples: `198.51.100.0/24`, `203.0.113.0/24`.
   - **AS Number**
     - Enter the Autonomous System (AS) number.
     - Valid ranges: `1-65534`, `131072-4294967294`.
     - Examples: `64512`, `200000`.
   - **Letter of Authorization (LOA)**
     - Upload the LOA document in PDF format.
     - Maximum file size: 10 MB.
     - The LOA must confirm authorization for the submitted network.
3. Submit the network.
   - Review the entered information.
   - Click **Add protected network** to submit the request.
   - The portal redirects to the **Protected networks** page.

   <Frame>
     ![Add protected network](/images/docs/ddos-protection/onboarding-service/custom-protection-profile-configuration/add-protected-network.png)
   </Frame>
4. Check the submission status.
   - The newly submitted network appears in the list with the **Pending** status until the network operations center (NOC) team reviews it.

   <Note>
     The following message appears:

     Your request is pending approval by our NOC team. We will notify you by email once the network is approved and ready for configuration.
   </Note>

   <Frame>
     ![Add protected network](/images/docs/ddos-protection/onboarding-service/custom-protection-profile-configuration/pending-approval.png)
   </Frame>

<Note>
  - All fields are required.
  - The LOA document must be accurate and up to date.
  - The network remains in **Pending** status until NOC approval.
</Note>

Once the network is approved, continue with [review and approval](/ddos-protection/onboarding-service/review-and-approval) to check the outcome.
