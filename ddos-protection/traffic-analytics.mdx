Traffic Analytics shows live traffic on resources with Advanced DDoS Protection. Use a time range or a destination IP to inspect volume, mitigation decisions, and top talkers.

The **Clean (P95)** card is the billing metric for Advanced protection. For the measurement period and billing rules, see [Plan activation](/ddos-protection/onboarding-service/plan-activation).

## Open Traffic Analytics

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Traffic Analytics">
    In the Customer Portal, navigate to **DDoS Protection** → **Reports** → **Traffic Analytics**.

    <Frame>![Traffic Analytics page with Last month time range and Search by IP address](/images/docs/ddos-protection/about-ddos-protection/traffic-analytics-filters.png)</Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Narrow the time range or destination IP">
    Select a time preset or a custom range. Use **Search by IP address** to filter by destination IP.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Summary cards and charts

Summary cards report **Inbound (Max)**, **Outbound (Max)**, **Blocked (Max)**, **Clean (P95)**, and **Event Count**. Charts below the cards show Traffic Overview, Policy Overview, Latest events, and Event Vectors.

<Frame>![Traffic Overview chart with Inbound, Clean, and Blocked series](/images/docs/ddos-protection/about-ddos-protection/traffic-overview.png)</Frame>

Traffic Overview plots inbound, clean, and blocked traffic over the selected window.

## Advanced Analytics

**Advanced Analytics** is on the same page. It breaks the selected window down by Top Source IPs, Top Destination IPs, Top Protocols, Source Countries, Destination Ports, Source Ports, and Packet Sizes.

Most of those views switch between bits per second (BPS) and packets per second (PPS). Top Protocols and Packet Sizes do not have a BPS/PPS toggle.
