# Account owner and ownership transfer

Customer change: An account has one owner, shown with an Owner badge, and that user can transfer ownership in the Customer Portal. An administrator cannot remove or demote the owner.
Affected users: Account owners and administrators.
Release evidence: not verified. The backend transfer is described as live on the IAM Confluence page. The portal screen (IMP-2140) and the badge (IMP-2357) are still in Backlog and were not used.
Open verification: Confirm the Owner badge, the Ownership delegation field, the confirmation screen, and the block on removing or demoting the owner in the Customer Portal.
