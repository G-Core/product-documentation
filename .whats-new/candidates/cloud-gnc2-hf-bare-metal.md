# High-frequency bare metal servers in Hong Kong

Customer change: GNC-2 (Hong Kong) offers two public high-frequency bare metal types, Intel Xeon E-2288G and Intel Xeon E-2388G, each with 8 cores, 128 GB RAM, and 2x 1 GbE. The E-2288G disks are 2x 500 GB SSD in RAID 1. The E-2388G disks are 2x 480 GB SSD in RAID 1.
Affected users: Customers ordering a Bare Metal server in Hong Kong.
Release evidence: On 2026-11-27 the production bare metal flavor list for region 64 (Hong Kong, GNC-2) returned bm1-hf-2288g-128-r1-2x500gb-2x1nic and bm2-hf-2388g-128-r1-2x500gb-2x1nic as Linux flavors with those hardware descriptions. GCLOUD2-29243 is Done; the owner wrote on 2026-11-11 that both flavors are public in GNC-2.
