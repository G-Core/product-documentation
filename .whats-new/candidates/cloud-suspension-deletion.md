# Cloud deletion after suspension

Customer change: After Cloud suspension, on any tariff, Gcore can delete Virtual Machines, Bare Metal servers, and GPU instances, together with data on local storage, at any time and without further notice. Network-attached resources (block volumes, file shares, and IP addresses) can be kept for up to 14 days and can be deleted at any time during or after that period, also without further notice.
Affected users: Customers whose Cloud services are suspended
Release evidence: Agreed clause from legal review on DOC-2050 (Rauf and Sasha, relayed 2026-10-20). Not observed on the Customer Portal. No effective date.
Open verification: Confirm the date this clause applies, and whether suspension already deletes instances and local storage at any time.
