[Gcore&nbsp;DDoS&nbsp;Protection](https://gcore.com/ddos-protection) detects and blocks malicious traffic before it reaches protected networks and servers. The service helps keep websites, applications, and network services available during DDoS attacks.

Gcore offers two protection modes: Basic and Advanced. They differ in attack coverage, detection speed, service availability during attacks, and cost.

| | Advanced protection | Basic protection |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pricing | Paid | Free |
| Attack detection | Under 1 second | 3 minutes |
| Attacks covered | Amplification, IP spoofing, volumetric (L3), connection (L4), and application-layer (L7) | Amplification and IP spoofing |
| During an attack | Traffic passes through a threat mitigation system, which drops the attack and forwards the rest | The attacked address is null-routed |
| Fits | Networks that are attacked often, or that must stay available, including against L7 attacks | Networks that are rarely attacked, or that can be unreachable while blocked |

Detection times in the table apply to network protection. [Bare&nbsp;Metal&nbsp;servers](/cloud/bare-metal-servers/advanced-ddos-protection/activate-advanced-ddos-protection-for-bare-metal-servers) and [dedicated&nbsp;servers](/hosting/other-services/ddos-protection/about-gcore-ddos-protection-for-dedicated-servers) detect an attack within 5 seconds and follow their own setup.

Basic protection uses predefined rules against reflection and IP spoofing attacks. When an attack is detected, the targeted IP address is null-routed for 12 hours.

The rule list and allowlist behavior are in [How&nbsp;protection&nbsp;works](/ddos-protection/about-ddos-protection).

Monitor protected traffic and review detected attacks in the Customer Portal.

* [Traffic&nbsp;Analytics](/ddos-protection/traffic-analytics) shows live volume, mitigation decisions, and top talkers on Advanced resources.
* [Events&nbsp;log](/ddos-protection/events-log) keeps incident records from the past three months.

## Pricing

Advanced protection plans are based on clean traffic volume and the number of protected network prefixes. Available tiers are in [Plan&nbsp;activation](/ddos-protection/onboarding-service/plan-activation).
