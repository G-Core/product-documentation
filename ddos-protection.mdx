[Gcore&nbsp;DDoS&nbsp;Protection](https://gcore.com/ddos-protection) detects and blocks malicious traffic before it reaches protected networks and servers. This reduces the impact of DDoS attacks on websites, applications, and network services.

Gcore offers two protection modes: Basic and Advanced. They differ in attack coverage, detection speed, service availability during attacks, and cost.

For [Bare&nbsp;Metal&nbsp;servers](/cloud/bare-metal-servers/advanced-ddos-protection/activate-advanced-ddos-protection-for-bare-metal-servers) and [dedicated&nbsp;servers](/hosting/other-services/ddos-protection/about-gcore-ddos-protection-for-dedicated-servers), the settings are separate and an attack is detected within 5 seconds.

| | Basic protection | Advanced protection |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pricing | Free | Paid |
| Attack detection | 3 minutes | Under 1 second |
| Attacks covered | Amplification<br />IP spoofing | Amplification<br />IP spoofing<br />Volumetric (L3)<br />Connection (L4)<br />Application-layer (L7) |
| During an attack | The attacked address is null-routed | Traffic passes through a threat mitigation system, which drops the attack and forwards the rest |
| Fits | Networks that are rarely attacked, or that can be unreachable while blocked | Networks that are attacked often, or that must stay available, including against L7 attacks |

## Basic protection

Basic protection uses [predefined&nbsp;rules](/ddos-protection/about-ddos-protection) to detect reflection and IP spoofing attacks. When an attack is detected, the targeted IP address is null-routed for 12 hours. This blocks traffic to the address, including legitimate requests, and makes services using that address unavailable during the block.

## Advanced protection

Advanced protection filters malicious traffic and forwards legitimate requests to protected networks. Unlike Basic protection, it allows services to remain accessible during an attack.

Select a plan and submit the network for approval to turn protection on. A custom profile is optional and replaces the profile already on that network.
