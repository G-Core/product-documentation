Gcore DDoS Protection safeguards servers and networks against distributed denial-of-service (DDoS) attacks. Advanced protection routes traffic through a threat mitigation system that filters attack traffic and forwards legitimate traffic to the protected infrastructure.

Set up and manage protection in the [Gcore Customer Portal](https://portal.gcore.com). The left-side menu covers:

  * [Overview](/ddos-protection/about-ddos-protection) - protection modes, pricing, and attack statistics
  * [Service activation](/ddos-protection/onboarding-service/service-activation) - plan activation, network setup, custom profiles, and GRE tunnel configuration
  * [Events log](/ddos-protection/events-log) - attack details from the past three months in the Customer Portal
