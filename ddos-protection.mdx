Gcore DDoS Protection safeguards servers and networks against distributed denial-of-service (DDoS) attacks. Basic protection is free and on by default, while Advanced protection is a paid add-on that routes traffic through a threat mitigation system, filters attack traffic, and forwards legitimate traffic to the protected infrastructure.

Set up and manage protection in the [Gcore Customer Portal](https://portal.gcore.com). These docs cover:

  * [Overview](/ddos-protection/about-ddos-protection) - protection modes and pricing
  * [Service activation](/ddos-protection/onboarding-service/service-activation) - plan activation, network setup, custom profiles, and GRE tunnel configuration
  * [Traffic Analytics](/ddos-protection/traffic-analytics) - live traffic, summary cards, and Advanced Analytics
  * [Events log](/ddos-protection/events-log) - attack details from the past three months in the Customer Portal
