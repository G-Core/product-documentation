title: "Task B: Fix a bad draft"
---

**Time:** 60 minutes

**What you receive:** A draft article prepared by a junior writer with AI assistance. The article covers a real topic but contains structural, logical, and language problems.

**The draft:** [Set up DNS for a web application](task-b-draft)

**What you deliver:**

1. A corrected version of the article.
2. A list of problems you found, with a brief explanation of why each is a problem.
3. A list of claims you could not verify, with a note on how you would check each one.
4. A short description of what you checked and how.

**Notes:**

- You can use AI, public documentation, search, and any other tools. AI use does not affect the evaluation.
- If you can't finish everything in 60 minutes, note what you would do next.
- Focus on what the text itself tells you — and what it fails to tell you.
