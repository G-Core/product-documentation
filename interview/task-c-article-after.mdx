Always Online reduces downtime impact by serving stale cached content when the origin is unavailable, helping maintain user access during outages. CDN switches back to live content automaticallу once the origin recovers. Stale responses include the `X-Cache: Stale` header.

CDN serves stale content for the error conditions listed in the portal.

## Enable Always Online

1. In the [Gcore Customer Portal](https://portal.gcore.com), navigate to **CDN** > **CDN resources** and open the resource settings.
2. In the sidebar, click **Cache**, then click **Always online**.
3. Enable the **Enable always online** toggle.
4. Enable all error conditions to maximize coverage, then click **Save changes**.

Always Online does not generate additional CDN traffic charges when serving stale content.
