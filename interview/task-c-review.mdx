---
title: "Task C: Review a colleague's edit"
---

**Time:** 30–45 minutes

**What you receive:** Two versions of the same article — a before and an after. A colleague edited the article and submitted it for review before publishing.

- [Before (original version)](task-c-article-before)
- [After (colleague's version)](task-c-article-after)

**What you deliver:**

1. For each change you find: state whether it is an improvement, a problem, or questionable — and explain why in one or two sentences.
2. For any change that introduces an error or removes important information: describe exactly what was lost and what you would do.
3. For any claim you cannot verify: note it and describe how you would check it.

**Notes:**

- You can use any tools, including AI.
- Focus on the quality of the edits, not on rewriting the whole article.
- It is fine to say that a change is good. Not every edit is a problem.
