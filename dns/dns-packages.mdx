Managed DNS plans are Free, Pro, and Enterprise. Free serves unlimited DNS requests with one dynamic record set per zone and one health check, while Pro raises the record and health-check limits and Enterprise adds vanity name servers and a white-label solution. Prices and overage rates are on the [pricing&nbsp;page](https://gcore.com/pricing/edge-network).
