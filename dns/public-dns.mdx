Gcore Public DNS is a free recursive resolver available to anyone without registration. Use it instead of the default resolver from your ISP or network to resolve domains through Gcore's global infrastructure and reduce exposure to DNS spoofing and substitution attacks.

DNS queries are anonymized, processed under EU jurisdiction, and not shared with third parties except as required by law. The service accepts up to 100 requests per second per client IP.

If you need to host authoritative DNS for a domain you own, use [Managed DNS](/dns) instead.

## Resolver addresses

| Protocol | Address |
|----------|---------|
| IPv4 | `95.85.95.85` or `2.56.220.2` |
| IPv6 | `2a03:90c0:999d::1` or `2a03:90c0:9992::1` |

To encrypt DNS queries in transit, see [Configure DNS over HTTPS and DNS over TLS](/dns/configure-doh-and-dot).
