A Gcore hosting reseller purchases servers and provides them to customers through its own billing system.

This program is separate from the reseller program managed through the [Admin&nbsp;Portal](/reseller-support), which resells CDN, Storage, and Streaming products under a company's own brand rather than a discount on server purchases.

To join the reseller program, [contact&nbsp;support](/hosting/contact-our-technical-support) from the existing Hosting Portal account and provide:

- Full company name
- Registration address
- Registration number
- Contact email
- CEO or director name
- The IP address from which requests to the Hosting Portal will be sent

No separate account is created. After approval, that account is added to the reseller group.

The group is not shown in the portal. The discount appears in the [Gcore&nbsp;Hosting&nbsp;Portal](https://hosting.gcore.com/billmgr) under **Client** > **Discounts** in the left sidebar.

Add funds to the account balance before orders start. New orders and renewals are charged from that balance. The calls that list tariffs and place orders are in [Billing&nbsp;calls](/hosting/manage-hosting-services-via-api).
