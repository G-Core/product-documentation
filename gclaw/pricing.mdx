Gclaw is a paid service currently under active development. Features, pricing, and limits may change upon reasonable prior notice.

## Plans

The Lite plan includes managed inference (no API keys required), Telegram and Discord integrations, and extra skills via Clawhub.

| Plan | Monthly price | Included tokens |
|------|---------------|-----------------|
| Lite | $6.90 / €5.99 | 30M input + 6M output |

Additional usage beyond the included bundle is billed at the following rates:

| | Rate |
|--|------|
| Input tokens | $0.11 / €0.10 per 1M |
| Output tokens | $0.21 / €0.20 per 1M |

## Trial

A 7-day trial is available once per account. The trial includes 8M input tokens and 2M output tokens. Activating the trial requires adding a payment method; cancelling before the trial ends incurs no charge.

## Beta conditions

During the beta period:

- Each account is limited to one Gclaw instance (one VM).
- Instances operate under a spot tariff: they may be suspended or terminated, including while in use, on at least 24 hours' prior notice.
- No service level agreements, guarantees, or support commitments apply.
