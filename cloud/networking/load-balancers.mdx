Gcore Load Balancers distribute traffic across Virtual Machines and Bare Metal servers. lb1 flavors provide different combinations of compute resources and are available in all Gcore Cloud regions. Network-optimised lb2 flavors provide guaranteed network bandwidth of up to 10 Gbps and are available in selected regions.

Load Balancers support HTTP, HTTPS, and long-lived connections through WebSockets, Server-Sent Events (SSE), and long polling. For long-lived connections, configure the data timeout to match application requirements.

## Performance analysis

The results below compare lb1 Load Balancer flavors under the same test conditions.

Each flavor was tested in multithreading mode with 36 concurrent threads and 400 connections over 30 seconds.

The results cover:

- Throughput: the number of requests per second (RPS) a Load Balancer can handle under concurrent load.
- Latency: response times for both HTTP and HTTPS traffic across different Load Balancer flavors.

| Flavor | HTTP Throughput (RPS) | HTTP Latency (ms) | HTTPS Throughput (RPS) | HTTPS Latency (ms) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 vCPU - 2 GiB | 21k | 4 | 20k | 20 |
| 2 vCPU - 4 GiB | 45k | 3 | 34k | 12 |
| 4 vCPU - 8 GiB | 91k | 5 | 51k | 8 |
| 8 vCPU - 16 GiB | 142k | 3 | 117k | 4 |

The results above apply to lb1 flavors. Network-optimised lb2 flavors run on separate dedicated infrastructure and are described below.

## Network optimised

Network-optimised Load Balancers (lb2 flavors) provide guaranteed network bandwidth through QoS policies, ranging from 1.5&nbsp;Gbps to 10&nbsp;Gbps depending on the selected flavor.

| Flavor | vCPU | RAM (GiB) | Network bandwidth |
| ---------- | -------- | ------------- | --------------------- |
| lb2-2-2    | 2        | 2             | 1.5&nbsp;Gbps         |
| lb2-4-4    | 4        | 4             | 5&nbsp;Gbps           |
| lb2-8-8    | 8        | 8             | 10&nbsp;Gbps          |
| lb2-16-16  | 16       | 16            | 10&nbsp;Gbps          |

lb2 flavors are currently available in Luxembourg-2 (ED-16), London-2 (NDLO-2), and Sines-3 (NDP-3). For availability in other regions, reach out to [Gcore&nbsp;Support](mailto:support@gcore.com).
