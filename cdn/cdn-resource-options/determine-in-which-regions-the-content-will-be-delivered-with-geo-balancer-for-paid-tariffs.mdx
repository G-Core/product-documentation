Geobalancer limits which CDN regions deliver content. Restricting delivery to the regions where most clients are located can increase the Cache Hit Ratio.

<Info>
Geobalancer is not available on the Free CDN plan. The [Gcore Customer Portal](https://portal.gcore.com) does not expose Geobalancer controls — [Gcore&nbsp;Support](mailto:support@gcore.com) configures it for the account. The setting applies to all CDN resources in the account.
</Info>

## Delivery regions

CDN edge servers are grouped into these regions:

- North America
- Latin America
- Asia
- Middle East
- Australia
- Africa
- Europe
- CIS
- World

**World** uses anycast addressing, so content can be served from any region in the global network when that region can deliver it most efficiently. When excluding specific regions, World must also be disabled — otherwise requests can still be served from excluded regions through anycast.

## Default location handler

Requests from disabled regions are routed to the default location handler. The handler must be one of the regions still enabled for delivery. Typical handler regions include:

- Asia
- Middle East
- Latin America
- Europe

The region selected as the default location handler cannot be disabled.

## Enable or change Geobalancer

Contact [Gcore&nbsp;Support](mailto:support@gcore.com) or use the chat in the Customer Portal. Provide the regions to enable or disable and the preferred default location handler.
