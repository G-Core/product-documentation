Gcore Video CDN delivers prepared HTTP Live Streaming (HLS), Dynamic Adaptive Streaming over HTTP (MPEG-DASH), HTTP Flash Video (HTTP-FLV), and MP4 content globally without transcoding. After setup, viewers request streams and files through a CDN domain while the CDN retrieves content from the configured origin.

Activation requirements differ by delivery format:

- **HLS and MPEG-DASH** — require video-delivery approval from Gcore Support and a paid CDN plan.
- **HTTP-FLV Live** — requires Support approval, a sales agreement, and a dedicated service profile. Contact your account manager or [sales@gcore.com](mailto:sales@gcore.com).
- **HTTP-FLV video on demand (VOD) and MP4 VOD** — self-service; only a paid CDN plan is required.

## Video CDN or Gcore Video Streaming

Select Video CDN when prepared streams already exist, or Gcore Video Streaming when transcoding and a hosted workflow are required.

### Video CDN

[Video&nbsp;CDN](/cdn/video-cdn/video-cdn-overview) delivers prepared content without transcoding and is suitable when:

- HLS, MPEG-DASH, HTTP-FLV, or MP4 content is available
- Global delivery is required
- Geo-blocking, secure token authentication, custom domains, or Origin Shielding is required
- Origin bandwidth must be reduced

It supports HLS `.m3u8` playlists with `.ts` or `.m4s` segments, MPEG-DASH `.mpd` manifests, HTTP-FLV `.flv` streams, and progressive MP4 downloads.

### Gcore Video Streaming

[Gcore&nbsp;Video&nbsp;Streaming](/streaming) accepts Real-Time Messaging Protocol (RTMP) or Secure Reliable Transport (SRT) input and transcodes it into adaptive-bitrate renditions. It also provides encoder integration, digital video recorder (DVR), and recording.

## Quick setup

Follow the steps below. The approval step applies to HLS, MPEG-DASH, and HTTP-FLV Live; HTTP-FLV VOD and MP4 VOD can proceed directly to resource creation. The LIVE STREAMING preset step applies to live delivery only.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Request video-delivery approval (HLS, MPEG-DASH, or HTTP-FLV Live)">
    <Warning>
    HLS and MPEG-DASH require video-delivery approval from Gcore Support and a paid CDN plan. HTTP-FLV Live requires Support approval, a sales agreement, and a dedicated service profile — contact your account manager or [sales@gcore.com](mailto:sales@gcore.com). HTTP-FLV VOD and MP4 VOD are self-service and do not require this step.
    </Warning>

    Contact [Gcore&nbsp;Support](mailto:support@gcore.com) or use the account chat to request prepared HLS or MPEG-DASH delivery for the CDN account.

    Provide:

    - **Client ID** (shown in the profile menu at the top right — click the avatar and look for **Client ID**)
    - Domain name and company name
    - Content type: sports, education, entertainment, or news
    - Sample stream URL: an example of the typical stream format
    - Viewer geography: where the audience is located
    - Broadcasting license or content compliance policy
    - Abuse or copyright contact email

    For sports, TV channels, events, or other third-party protected content, Support may request proof that the account holder or its clients have rights to distribute the content. Requests without the necessary licensing details, company identity, or abuse handling information may be declined or closed.

    Processing time is usually 1–2 business days.

    <Frame>![Profile menu at the top right showing the Client ID](/images/docs/cdn/getting-started/create-a-cdn-resource/create-a-cdn-resource-for-video-streaming/account-id-profile-menu.png)</Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Activate a paid CDN plan">
    Video streaming requires a paid CDN subscription.

    1. Navigate to **CDN** in the [Gcore&nbsp;Customer&nbsp;Portal](https://portal.gcore.com).

    2. On the free plan, upgrade to a paid CDN plan. See [CDN&nbsp;billing](/cdn/how-the-cdn-service-and-its-additional-options-are-billed) for current plan options and included traffic allowances.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create a CDN resource">
    After approval for HLS or MPEG-DASH — or immediately for HTTP-FLV or MP4 — create the CDN resource:

    1. Navigate to **CDN** > [CDN&nbsp;resources](https://portal.gcore.com/cdn/resources/list).

    2. Click **Create CDN resource**.

    3. Enter the domain that will serve the Video CDN — for instance, `video.example.com`. Use **Add CNAME** to add more domains if needed.

    4. Under **Origin**, keep **Specify content origin** selected and configure one source:
       - URL — enter a resolvable media server domain or IP in **Origin source** — `live.example.com`
       - S3 storage — select the Gcore Storage bucket that contains the VOD files
       - FastEdge — select the FastEdge app that generates the response

    5. Click **Create**.

    For each [custom&nbsp;domain](/cdn/cdn-resource-options/general/create-and-set-a-custom-domain-for-the-content-delivery-via-cdn), create a CNAME record that points to the assigned CDN domain and issue or upload a valid SSL certificate before testing over HTTPS.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Configure caching rules">
    Optimize caching for the delivery type. The settings below are organized by delivery format.

    <Tabs>
      <Tab title="HLS live">
        **Baseline cache settings:**

        1. Navigate to **CDN Resource** > **Cache**.

        2. Enable **Always online** and add these error types:
           - error
           - timeout

        When an error or timeout occurs, Always Online serves previously cached content while a valid stale response is available.

        <Warning>
        Do not add `updating` to the error types list for live streaming. Add only `error` and `timeout`.
        </Warning>

        3. Set **CDN cache expiry time** to 60 seconds. With DVR enabled, increase this value to match the DVR window.

        4. Click **Save changes**.

        **Manifest caching rule:**

        5. Navigate to **CDN Resource** > **Rules** and click **Create rule**.

        <Frame>![Create rule dropdown with the Playlists and Video segments templates](/images/docs/cdn/getting-started/create-a-cdn-resource/create-a-cdn-resource-for-video-streaming/create-rule-dropdown.png)</Frame>

        6. Select **Playlists template (.m3u8)**.

        7. Set **Cache time** to 2 seconds and **Browser cache** to 0 seconds, then click **Create rule**.

        A 60 s segment cache and 2 s manifest cache balance segment availability with playlist freshness.

        **Low-Latency HLS (LL-HLS) query parameters:**

        LL-HLS requires its delivery parameters in the cache key.

        8. Navigate to **CDN Resource** > **Cache**.

        9. Enable **Query parameters allowlist**.

        10. Add these parameters: `_HLS_msn`, `_HLS_part`, `_HLS_skip`.
            - These are required for LL-HLS to work correctly
            - Other parameters are excluded from the cache key but are still forwarded to the origin

        11. Click **Save changes**.
      </Tab>
      <Tab title="MPEG-DASH live">
        **Baseline cache settings:**

        1. Navigate to **CDN Resource** > **Cache**.

        2. Enable **Always online** and add these error types:
           - error
           - timeout

        When an error or timeout occurs, Always Online serves previously cached content while a valid stale response is available.

        <Warning>
        Do not add `updating` to the error types list for live streaming. Add only `error` and `timeout`.
        </Warning>

        3. Set **CDN cache expiry time** to 60 seconds. With DVR enabled, increase this value to match the DVR window.

        4. Click **Save changes**.

        **Manifest caching rule:**

        5. Navigate to **CDN Resource** > **Rules** and click **Create rule**.

        6. Select **Create blank rule** and use `^/.+\.mpd$` as the pattern.

        7. Set **Cache time** to 2 seconds and **Browser cache** to 0 seconds, then click **Create rule**.

        **Low-Latency DASH (LL-DASH) with Common Media Application Format (CMAF) segments:**

        Low-latency MPEG-DASH with an external transcoder and CMAF segments requires the Chunked-Proxy module.

        <Warning>
        When using MPEG-DASH with an external transcoder (not Gcore Video Streaming), enable the Chunked-Proxy module for low-latency delivery by contacting Gcore Support or the account manager.

        Without Chunked-Proxy, the CDN uses store-and-forward delivery, which can increase latency.
        </Warning>

        **Chunked-Proxy module**

        Standard CDNs wait for files to download fully from origin before sending them to viewers. The Chunked-Proxy module sends bytes to viewers while receiving them from the origin, using chunked transfer encoding and protocol framing.

        The [low-latency&nbsp;streaming](/streaming/live-streaming/how-low-latency-streaming-works) configuration targets 2–4 seconds. Actual end-to-end latency depends on the transcoder, segment duration, player buffering, and network conditions.

        **Chunked-Proxy activation**

        1. Contact Gcore Support or the account manager.
        2. Request Chunked-Proxy module activation for the CDN resource.
        3. Provide the CDN resource ID or domain, confirmation of using LL-DASH with CMAF segments, and origin transcoder details (Wowza or Nginx-RTMP).

        After activation, the CDN streams chunks as they arrive from the origin instead of waiting for complete segments.

        <Info>
        When using Gcore Video Streaming (the Gcore transcoding service), Chunked-Proxy is already enabled by default. Activation is only needed for an external transcoder.
        </Info>

        Common DASH/CMAF file extensions:

        - Manifests: `.mpd`
        - Segments: `.m4s`, `.m4v`, `.m4a`, `.cmfv`, `.cmfa`
        - Initialization: `.mp4` (init segments)
      </Tab>
      <Tab title="HLS/MPEG-DASH VOD">
        VOD cache duration depends on whether content URLs are immutable or reused.

        **Enable Always online:**

        1. Navigate to **CDN Resource** > **Cache**.

        2. Enable **Always online** and add these error types:
           - error (serve stale cache if origin returns errors)
           - timeout (serve stale cache if origin times out)

        When an error or timeout occurs, Always Online serves previously cached video while a valid stale response is available.

        3. Click **Save changes**.

        **Set long-term cache:**

        4. Still in **CDN Resource** > **Cache**, select **CDN controlled** mode.

        5. For immutable or versioned URLs, set **CDN cache expiry time** to 1 year (31,536,000 seconds).
           - In CDN-controlled mode, responses with status codes 200, 206, 301, and 302 are cached by default; responses with other codes are not cached unless configured separately
           - In origin-controlled mode, caching is governed by the `Cache-Control` header the origin sends
           - For content replaced at the same URL, use a shorter TTL or [purge&nbsp;the&nbsp;cache](/cdn/purge-the-cdn-cache) after replacement

        6. Click **Save changes**.

        7. To limit caching of 404 responses, add an advanced caching rule: in **CDN caching** settings, click **Add rule**, set **Response code** to `404`, and set **Caching time** to 1 second. Click **Save changes**. This protects the origin from repeated requests for non-existent files.

        A 1-year TTL is appropriate only when content URLs are immutable or versioned. After replacing content at the same URL, [purge&nbsp;the&nbsp;cache](/cdn/purge-the-cdn-cache) to remove the older object.

        **(Optional) Query string handling:**

        8. Navigate to **CDN Resource** > **Cache**.

        9. Enable **Ignore query string** if VOD URLs do not use query parameters for logic, and caching `video.mp4?ref=email` and `video.mp4?ref=social` as the same file is preferred.

        10. Before removing query parameters from the cache key, confirm that they are not required for analytics or access control. Ignored parameters are still forwarded to the origin.

        11. Click **Save changes**.

        Common file extensions:

        - Manifests: `.m3u8`, `.mpd`
        - Segments: `.ts`, `.m4s`, `.m4v`, `.m4a`, `.m4i`
        - Subtitles: `.vtt`, `.webvtt`, `.ttml`
      </Tab>
      <Tab title="HTTP-FLV Live">
        [HTTP-FLV Live streaming](/cdn/video-cdn/flv-streaming) uses a single continuous connection and requires a dedicated service profile. Cache settings for HTTP-FLV Live are configured by Gcore Support as part of the service activation process — contact Support or your account manager to request the configuration.
      </Tab>
      <Tab title="HTTP-FLV VOD and MP4">
        **Enable Always online:**

        1. Navigate to **CDN Resource** > **Cache**.

        2. Enable **Always online** and add these error types:
           - error (serve stale cache if origin returns errors)
           - timeout (serve stale cache if origin times out)

        3. Click **Save changes**.

        **Set long-term cache:**

        4. Still in **CDN Resource** > **Cache**, select **CDN controlled** mode.

        5. For immutable or versioned URLs, set **CDN cache expiry time** to 1 year (31,536,000 seconds).
           - In CDN-controlled mode, responses with status codes 200, 206, 301, and 302 are cached; other codes require additional configuration
           - For content replaced at the same URL, use a shorter TTL or [purge&nbsp;the&nbsp;cache](/cdn/purge-the-cdn-cache) after replacement

        6. Click **Save changes**.

        7. To limit caching of 404 responses, add an advanced caching rule: in **CDN caching** settings, click **Add rule**, set **Response code** to `404`, and set **Caching time** to 1 second. Click **Save changes**.

        **(Optional) Large file optimization for MP4 progressive download:**

        8. Navigate to **CDN Resource** > **Content**.

        9. Enable **Large files delivery optimization**.
           - Retrieves content from the origin in 10 MB chunks using HTTP range requests and caches each chunk independently — intended for large single-file MP4 content
           - Generally unnecessary for segmented HLS or MPEG-DASH, which already divide media into small independently cached chunks
           - The origin must support HTTP Range requests
           - Enabling this invalidates existing cache
           - Incompatible with Fetch Compressed, Gzip compression, and Brotli compression

        10. Click **Save changes**.

        **(Optional) Query string handling:**

        11. Navigate to **CDN Resource** > **Cache**.

        12. Enable **Ignore query string** if VOD URLs do not use query parameters for logic.

        13. Before removing query parameters from the cache key, confirm that they are not required for analytics or access control. Ignored parameters are still forwarded to the origin.

        14. Click **Save changes**.

        Common file extensions:

        - Progressive: `.mp4`, `.webm`, `.flv`
        - Subtitles: `.vtt`, `.webvtt`, `.ttml`
      </Tab>
    </Tabs>

    <Info>
    For high-traffic streaming or specific performance requirements, contact Gcore Support to configure cache sharding across multiple servers, cache lock timing for concurrent requests, custom TTL strategies per file type, and origin connection limits that protect the origin from traffic spikes.
    </Info>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enable the LIVE STREAMING preset">
    <Info>
    For live streams only — skip this step when delivering VOD.
    </Info>

    The LIVE STREAMING preset enables RAM-based caching for reduced latency.

    1. Navigate to CDN resources.

    2. Click **⋯** (more options) next to the Video CDN resource.

    3. Select **Turn on LIVE STREAMING preset**.

    <Frame>![Resource menu with Turn on LIVE STREAMING preset](/images/docs/cdn/getting-started/create-a-cdn-resource/create-a-cdn-resource-for-video-streaming/live-streaming-preset-menu.png)</Frame>

    It caches video segments in RAM instead of disk for faster delivery and lower latency.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Video delivery testing

Confirm that the HTTPS CDN URL plays correctly before going to production.

### CDN URL

Replace the origin hostname with the CDN hostname.

Before (origin):

```text
https://live.example.com/playlist.m3u8
```

After (CDN):

```text
https://video.example.com/playlist.m3u8
```

Or with an auto-generated domain:

```text
https://123456.gcdn.co/playlist.m3u8
```

### Playback test

Select a playback check that matches the delivery format:

- HLS — enter the CDN URL in the [HLS.js&nbsp;demo](https://hls-js.netlify.app/demo/) or a native HLS player, then verify that the playlist and segments load
- MPEG-DASH — [VLC&nbsp;Media&nbsp;Player](https://videolan.org) and other MPEG-DASH players can open the `.mpd` URL; verify continuous playback
- HTTP-FLV — open the `.flv` URL in an HTTP-FLV player and verify that the continuous response plays
- MP4 — open the CDN URL in a browser or desktop player, then verify playback and seeking

## Advanced configuration

After the resource is live, Origin Shielding, geo-restrictions, and secure tokens can further protect the origin and control who can watch.

### Origin Shielding

Origin Shielding routes cache misses through a designated shield location before requests reach the origin, consolidating requests and reducing direct origin traffic.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Origin Shielding settings">
    Navigate to **CDN Resource** > **General** > **Origin shielding**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable and select a location">
    Toggle **Enable origin shielding**, then select the shield location closest to the origin. Click **Save changes**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The [Origin&nbsp;Shielding](/cdn/cdn-resource-options/general/enable-and-configure-origin-shielding) settings support location selection and failover, so select a shield location close to the origin.

### Geo-restrictions

[Geo-restrictions](/cdn/cdn-resource-options/security/control-access-to-the-content-with-country-referrer-ip-and-user-agents-policies#country-access-policy) combine country access with referrer, IP, and user-agent rules.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the country access policy">
    Navigate to **CDN Resource** > **Security** > **Country access policy**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select allow or block">
    Select **Allow only** to make content available only in selected countries, or **Block** to block content in selected countries.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply the countries">
    Select countries, then save changes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Example: "Allow only in Dominican Republic" for regional sports rights.

### Secure tokens

[Secure&nbsp;Tokens](/cdn/cdn-resource-options/security/use-a-secure-token/about-secure-token) protect paid content, private videos, and time-limited previews with authenticated URLs.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Secure Token settings">
    Navigate to **CDN Resource** > **Security** > **Secure Token**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure and generate tokens">
    Enable and configure token settings, then click **Save changes**. Generate tokens on the backend for each user.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Troubleshooting

Use checks that match the delivery format and determine whether failure occurs at the origin, CDN, DNS, or player. Contact Gcore Support or use the account chat if the issue remains.

### Playback failures

Check the origin, domain, media files, and CDN response in order.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Test the origin directly">
    Request the origin URL directly and confirm it returns the expected content. When testing virtual-hosted, authenticated, or private origins, the test must preserve the expected Host header, TLS server name indication (SNI), any authentication credentials, and network access — a test that omits these may succeed even when the CDN request fails.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify the custom domain">
    For a custom domain, confirm that its CNAME points to the CDN domain and that the SSL certificate is valid.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the format-specific CDN response">
    Verify the format-specific response from the CDN:
    - HLS — verify the `.m3u8` playlist and referenced `.ts` or `.m4s` segments
    - MPEG-DASH — verify the `.mpd` manifest and referenced `.m4s` segments
    - HTTP-FLV — verify that the `.flv` endpoint maintains a continuous HTTP response
    - MP4 — verify a successful response and HTTP Range support for seeking
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check CDN statistics">
    Check 4xx and 5xx responses in [CDN&nbsp;statistics](/cdn/view-statistics-of-a-cdn-resource).
  </Step>
</Steps>

### High live-stream latency

For HLS or MPEG-DASH latency, review the live-delivery settings before requesting workload-specific tuning.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Confirm the LIVE STREAMING preset is enabled">
    Confirm that the **LIVE STREAMING preset** is enabled on the CDN resource.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check manifest cache time">
    Check that manifest cache time is appropriate for the stream — 2 seconds is recommended for live delivery.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify Chunked-Proxy for LL-DASH">
    For Low-Latency DASH (LL-DASH) with an external transcoder, confirm that Chunked-Proxy is active.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Contact Support for tuning">
    Contact Gcore Support for workload-specific tuning.
  </Step>
</Steps>
