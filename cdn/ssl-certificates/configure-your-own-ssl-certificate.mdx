A custom SSL certificate must be issued for the [custom domain](/cdn/cdn-resource-options/general/create-and-set-a-custom-domain-for-the-content-delivery-via-cdn) of the CDN resource, such as `cdn.example.com`. A wildcard certificate covers all subdomains, such as `*.example.com`.

Upload the certificate in the [Gcore Customer Portal](https://portal.gcore.com), then attach it when creating a resource or on an existing resource.

## Upload an SSL certificate

Open **CDN** > **SSL certificates** and click **Add SSL certificate**.

<Frame>![SSL certificates list with Add SSL certificate](/images/docs/cdn/ssl-certificates/configure-your-own-ssl-certificate/own-ssl-1.png)</Frame>

The create form opens.

<Frame>![Create SSL Certificate form with Name, Certificate, and Private key](/images/docs/cdn/ssl-certificates/configure-your-own-ssl-certificate/ssl-certificate-data.png)</Frame>

Enter the certificate data from the certificate authority:

- **Name**: a label used only in the Customer Portal.
- **Certificate**: the certificate chain in [PEM format](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Privacy-Enhanced_Mail) (personal certificate, intermediate CA, root CA).
- **Private key**: the matching private key.

Paste the chain and key as described in [Certificate chain and key](#certificate-chain-and-key). Leave **Signed by a trusted CA.** checked when the certificate is from a trusted CA. Clear the checkbox when it is not.

Click **Create SSL Certificate**. The certificate appears in the SSL certificates list.

<Frame>![SSL certificate in the certificates list](/images/docs/cdn/ssl-certificates/configure-your-own-ssl-certificate/own-ssl-3.png)</Frame>

Attach the certificate to a CDN resource after upload. An uploaded certificate is not used until it is selected on a resource.

### Certificate chain and key

Open the certificate file in PEM format (`.pem`, `.crt`, or `.cer`) in a text editor.

Copy the chain in this order: personal certificate, intermediate CA, root CA. Paste the full contents into **Certificate**, including the `-----BEGIN CERTIFICATE-----` and `-----END CERTIFICATE-----` tags. Do not leave blank lines between certificates.

<Frame>![Certificate chain pasted into the Certificate field](/images/docs/cdn/ssl-certificates/configure-your-own-ssl-certificate/own-ssl-4.png)</Frame>

Add a new line at the end of the chain.

<Frame>![New line at the end of the certificate chain](/images/docs/cdn/ssl-certificates/configure-your-own-ssl-certificate/own-ssl-5.png)</Frame>

Open the private key file (`.key`) in a text editor. Paste the full contents into **Private key**, including the `-----BEGIN PRIVATE KEY-----` and `-----END PRIVATE KEY-----` tags.

## SSL certificate on a CDN resource

A custom certificate can be attached during resource creation or on an existing resource.

### During resource creation

Follow the [static assets](/cdn/getting-started/create-a-cdn-resource/create-a-cdn-resource) flow until **Custom domain** is filled in.

<Frame>![Custom domain on the create CDN resource form](/images/docs/cdn/ssl-certificates/configure-your-own-ssl-certificate/own-ssl-6.png)</Frame>

Turn on **Enable HTTPS** and select **Custom SSL certificate**. If the certificate is already uploaded, select it from the list and finish resource creation.

<Accordion title="Certificate not uploaded yet">
Click **Add SSL certificate**, paste the chain and key in the dialog, then click **Add SSL certificate**. Use the same paste rules as Certificate chain and key.

<Frame>![Add SSL certificate dialog during resource creation](/images/docs/cdn/ssl-certificates/configure-your-own-ssl-certificate/ssl-cert-adding.png)</Frame>
</Accordion>

### Existing CDN resource

Open **CDN** > **CDN resources** and click the resource name.

<Frame>![CDN resources list](/images/docs/cdn/ssl-certificates/configure-your-own-ssl-certificate/own-ssl-8.png)</Frame>

On the **OPTIONS** tab, open **SSL** under **General**. Turn on **Enable HTTPS** and select **Custom SSL certificate**. If a certificate is already uploaded, select it from the list and click **Save changes**. If none are uploaded, click **Add SSL certificate**.

<Frame>![Enable HTTPS and Custom SSL certificate on an existing resource](/images/docs/cdn/ssl-certificates/configure-your-own-ssl-certificate/own-ssl-10.png)</Frame>

<Accordion title="Certificate not uploaded yet">
Click **Add SSL certificate**, paste the chain and key in the dialog, then click **Add SSL certificate**. Use the same paste rules as Certificate chain and key.

<Frame>![Add SSL certificate dialog on an existing resource](/images/docs/cdn/ssl-certificates/configure-your-own-ssl-certificate/ssl-cert-adding.png)</Frame>
</Accordion>

## Certificate management

Uploaded certificates are listed under **CDN** > **SSL certificates**. The table shows ID, name, connected CDN resources, expiration date, and start date.

<Frame>![SSL certificates table with ID, name, resources, and dates](/images/docs/cdn/ssl-certificates/configure-your-own-ssl-certificate/own-ssl-11.png)</Frame>

### SSL certificate renewal

Certificate data cannot be edited after upload. Before expiration, upload a new certificate, open the resource **SSL** settings, select the new certificate, and click **Save changes**. Wait at least 15 minutes.

To confirm the new certificate is bound, open `https://cdn.example.com` in a browser, click the lock icon, open **Connection is secure**, then **Certificate is valid**.

<Frame>![Browser certificate viewer for the custom domain](/images/docs/cdn/ssl-certificates/configure-your-own-ssl-certificate/own-ssl-12.png)</Frame>

Compare the displayed data with the new certificate. If the values match, [delete](#delete-an-ssl-certificate) the old certificate. Delete the old certificate only after content is served with the new one, because removing it too early interrupts HTTPS delivery.

### Expiration notifications

Email goes to administrator and engineer addresses 14 days before expiry, 7 days before expiry, and on the expiry date. A reminder also appears on login during those 14 days and after expiry. **SSL certificates** in the side panel shows an exclamation mark when a certificate is expired or will expire within 14 days.

<Frame>![SSL certificates menu with an expiration warning](/images/docs/cdn/ssl-certificates/configure-your-own-ssl-certificate/own-ssl-13.png)</Frame>

A red exclamation mark marks an expired certificate.

<Frame>![Expired SSL certificate warning in the list](/images/docs/cdn/ssl-certificates/configure-your-own-ssl-certificate/own-ssl-14.png)</Frame>

A yellow warning marks a certificate that expires in 14 days or fewer.

<Frame>![SSL certificate expiring soon warning in the list](/images/docs/cdn/ssl-certificates/configure-your-own-ssl-certificate/own-ssl-15.png)</Frame>

### Delete an SSL certificate

Click the three dots next to the certificate, then click **Delete**.

<Frame>![Delete action on an SSL certificate row](/images/docs/cdn/ssl-certificates/configure-your-own-ssl-certificate/own-ssl-16.png)</Frame>

A certificate that is attached to a CDN resource cannot be deleted. Open **SSL** on that resource and turn off **Enable HTTPS** first, then delete the certificate.
