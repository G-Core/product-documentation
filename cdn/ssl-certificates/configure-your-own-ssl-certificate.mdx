A custom SSL certificate must be issued for the [custom&nbsp;domain](/cdn/cdn-resource-options/general/create-and-set-a-custom-domain-for-the-content-delivery-via-cdn) of the CDN resource (`cdn.example.com`). A wildcard certificate covers all subdomains, including `*.example.com`.

Upload the certificate in the [Gcore Customer Portal](https://portal.gcore.com), then attach it on an existing CDN resource, because custom SSL is not selected on the create-resource form.

## Upload an SSL certificate

Open **CDN** > **SSL certificates** and click **Add SSL certificate**.

<Frame>![SSL certificates list with Add SSL certificate](/images/docs/cdn/ssl-certificates/configure-your-own-ssl-certificate/configure-your-own-ssl-certificate-image1.png)</Frame>

The **Create SSL Certificate** form opens.

<Frame>![Create SSL Certificate form with Name, Certificate, and Private key](/images/docs/cdn/ssl-certificates/configure-your-own-ssl-certificate/configure-your-own-ssl-certificate-image2.png)</Frame>

Enter the certificate data from the certificate authority:

- **Name**: a label used only in the Customer Portal.
- **Certificate**: the certificate chain in [PEM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Privacy-Enhanced_Mail) format (personal certificate, intermediate CA, root CA).
- **Private key**: the matching private key.

Paste the chain and key as described in [Certificate&nbsp;chain](#certificate-chain-and-key). Leave **Signed by a trusted CA.** checked when the certificate is from a trusted CA. Clear the checkbox when it is not.

<Frame>![Create SSL Certificate form filled with name, certificate chain, and private key](/images/docs/cdn/ssl-certificates/configure-your-own-ssl-certificate/configure-your-own-ssl-certificate-image3.png)</Frame>

Click **Create SSL Certificate**. The certificate appears in the SSL certificates list.

<Frame>![SSL certificate in the certificates list](/images/docs/cdn/ssl-certificates/configure-your-own-ssl-certificate/configure-your-own-ssl-certificate-image1.png)</Frame>

Attach the certificate to a CDN resource after upload, because an uploaded certificate is not used until it is selected on a resource.

### Certificate chain and key

Open the certificate file in PEM format (`.pem`, `.crt`, or `.cer`) in a text editor.

Copy the chain in this order: personal certificate, intermediate CA, root CA. Paste the full contents into **Certificate**, including the `-----BEGIN CERTIFICATE-----` and `-----END CERTIFICATE-----` tags. Do not leave blank lines between certificates.

<Frame>![Certificate chain pasted into the Certificate field](/images/docs/cdn/ssl-certificates/configure-your-own-ssl-certificate/configure-your-own-ssl-certificate-image4.png)</Frame>

Add a new line at the end of the chain.

Open the private key file (`.key`) in a text editor. Paste the full contents into **Private key**, including the `-----BEGIN PRIVATE KEY-----` and `-----END PRIVATE KEY-----` tags.

## SSL certificate on a CDN resource

Create the [CDN&nbsp;resource](/cdn/getting-started/create-a-cdn-resource/create-a-cdn-resource) first. On the create form, enter the hostname in **Add domain**. After the resource exists, attach the custom certificate in the resource SSL settings.

Open **CDN** > **CDN resources** and click the resource **CNAME**.

<Frame>![CDN resources list with CNAME column](/images/docs/cdn/ssl-certificates/configure-your-own-ssl-certificate/configure-your-own-ssl-certificate-image6.png)</Frame>

On the **OPTIONS** tab, open **SSL** under **General**. Turn on **Enable HTTPS** and select **Custom SSL certificate**. If a certificate is already uploaded, select it in the certificate dropdown and click **Save changes**. If none are uploaded, click **Add SSL certificate**.

<Frame>![Enable HTTPS and Custom SSL certificate on an existing resource](/images/docs/cdn/ssl-certificates/configure-your-own-ssl-certificate/configure-your-own-ssl-certificate-image7.png)</Frame>

<Accordion title="Certificate not uploaded yet">
Click **Add SSL certificate**, paste the chain and key in the dialog, then click **Add SSL certificate**. Use the same paste rules as Certificate chain and key.

<Frame>![Add SSL certificate dialog on an existing resource](/images/docs/cdn/ssl-certificates/configure-your-own-ssl-certificate/configure-your-own-ssl-certificate-image8.png)</Frame>
</Accordion>

## Certificate management

Uploaded certificates are listed under **CDN** > **SSL certificates**. The table shows ID, name, connected CDN resources, start date, and expiration date.

<Frame>![SSL certificates table with ID, name, resources, start, and expire](/images/docs/cdn/ssl-certificates/configure-your-own-ssl-certificate/configure-your-own-ssl-certificate-image1.png)</Frame>

### SSL certificate renewal

The certificate name can be changed from **Settings** on the certificate row, while the certificate and private key values cannot be edited after upload. Before expiration, upload a new certificate, open the resource **SSL** settings, select the new certificate, and click **Save changes**. Wait at least 15 minutes.

To confirm the new certificate is bound, open `https://cdn.example.com` in a browser, click the lock icon, open **Connection is secure**, then **Certificate is valid**.

Compare the displayed data with the new certificate. If the values match, [delete](#delete-an-ssl-certificate) the old certificate. Delete the old certificate only after content is served with the new one, because removing it too early interrupts HTTPS delivery.

### Expiration notifications

Email goes to administrator and engineer addresses 14 days before expiry, seven days before expiry, and on the expiry date. A reminder also appears on login during those 14 days and after expiry. **SSL certificates** in the side panel shows an exclamation mark when a certificate is expired or will expire within 14 days.

A red exclamation mark marks an expired certificate, while a yellow warning marks a certificate that expires in 14 days or fewer.

### Delete an SSL certificate

Click the three dots next to the certificate, then click **Delete**. The same menu includes **Settings**.

<Frame>![Settings and Delete actions on an SSL certificate row](/images/docs/cdn/ssl-certificates/configure-your-own-ssl-certificate/configure-your-own-ssl-certificate-image5.png)</Frame>

A confirmation dialog opens. Click **Yes, delete**.

A certificate that is attached to a CDN resource cannot be deleted. Open **SSL** on that resource and turn off **Enable HTTPS** first, then delete the certificate.
